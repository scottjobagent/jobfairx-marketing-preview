Employer marketing pages

Two prototypes and everything you need to build them: the employer homepage content update, and a redesigned hiring event details page. Read the two flagged items below before you start — both look like mistakes and neither is one.

Read this first

  • The live site's header and footer win. Both prototypes carry a capture of the current live chrome. Keep the live header and footer exactly as they are, desktop and mobile, and apply only the page content between them. Any header or footer oddity you spot in a prototype (including the mobile footer's hidden column labels) is an artifact of the capture. Do not port it.
  • Never edit the prototype HTML by hand. Both pages are generated. Edit the builder and re-run it. The homepage comes from build-employer-home.py; the event page from build-event-healthcare.py.
  • Two controls are prototype-only and must be deleted before production. The Desktop / Mobile toggle at the bottom-left of the homepage, and the lifecycle preview bar across the top of the event details page. Both are labelled in the markup.
  • Numbers are real or they are placeholders. Nothing on either page is invented. Where a figure has to come from the platform it is marked as dynamic below.
Looks broken, is not

The hero YouTube video reports as unplayable to any script or unauthenticated fetch — oembed returns 403, and a cookie-less render shows “This video is private”. It plays correctly in a real browser. The video is unlisted, and that is what an anonymous request sees. Check it in a browser, never in a script.

Needs a decision before launch

Early registration is 30 days or more before the event, per Scott. The live server currently computes 31 days, so it shows a deadline one day earlier than the approved copy. Verified at the boundary on real events: the Save $100 badge is present at 31 days out and gone at 30. Either the code moves to 30 or the copy moves to 31, but they must match.

Employer homepage

A content update to jobfairx.com/employer, not a redesign of the site chrome. The build diffs are all in build-employer-home.py; read its section comments top to bottom. Every step asserts against the captured DOM, so it fails loudly rather than silently.

What changed against the live page

  • Titles and meta drop “Virtual”. The category claim is “Hiring Event Platform for Employers”.
  • New hero copy: “Meet interview-ready candidates at hiring events built to hire.” with “Post the roles you need to fill, meet candidates matched to your openings, and interview them in person, by video, or by phone.”
  • How It Works: registration first; the Event day step carries the Interview Settings panel; Review and confirm uses real product screenshots that swap at the lg breakpoint.
  • All Packages Include reduced to 8 items, matching the pricing page word for word.
  • New section: Reschedule and follow-ups, replacing the live site's scroll-pinned Built-in Tools block. Two columns, copy and three points left, the employer tutorial right.

The reschedule section

  • The headline must not contain a <br>. An explicit break creates a phantom line box and the heading renders on three lines at every size down to 32px. Left to wrap naturally at lg:text-[40px] it breaks after “and”, which is the intended break. Verified by counting client rects.
  • There is no supporting paragraph by design. The three points say the same thing.
  • The section carries border-b border-slate-200 because it sits between two white bands and would otherwise run into Hiring Event Results.
  • The play button, progress bar and “1:04” are a designed still, not a live player. Clicking swaps in a native <video controls autoplay>.

Wiring you need to do

  • Mobile hamburger: the drawer does not open in the prototype. Wire it per the live site.
  • The page ships exactly one inline script, seven lines, at the end of the body. Its only job is the tutorial hand-off. Everything else is static.
  • The three Reserve My Spot buttons and the Growth card's stepper are inert. Production behaviour should match the live pricing page: rungs 1–4 then 5/10/15/25/40/50/75/100; cart URL uses bundle=<id> at bundle counts, otherwise pkg plus qty.
  • Dead anchors: “See how it works” in the Review and confirm step is href="#". The calendar mock's rows are illustrative and should render from the real event feed.
  • Analytics, JSON-LD and tracking were stripped at capture. Production keeps the live site's own.

Event details page

A redesign, not a clone. The live event page repeats the homepage: measured across the built Dallas clone, about 79% of its visible copy is identical on every one of the 1,760 event pages the template renders, and only about 6% changes per event. This version answers “why this event” instead.

Built by build-event-healthcare.py. The real event is Austin, TX Healthcare, 30 September 2026. The EV dictionary at the top of the builder is the single parameterisation point for the other four event types and the city pages.

Structure

  • Lifecycle banner, then a hero that leads with what the employer gets, with a registration card that sticks down the scroll. Price is deliberately a small link, not the headline.
  • The room so far — live candidate count plus the type's show rate and per-employer averages.
  • Who you will meet, with the ~20 mile verification stated for the city.
  • How it works in three steps, each with a real product screenshot: the review screen, the live event-day lobby, and the post-event report.
  • Packages priced by lifecycle state, one type-matched testimonial, an event-scoped FAQ, and the close.

The eight lifecycle states

Captured by rendering fifteen real event pages at 0, 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 28, 29, 30, 31, 36 and 45 days out. These strings and colours are verbatim from production. The preview bar at the top of the prototype exists so you can see all eight; delete it, its styles and the PREVIEW array before production. Production renders exactly one state, computed from the event date.

Data that must come from the platform

  • Registered candidate count — already live per event, with the “Updated daily” stamp. Do not hard-code it; the same template renders 1,760 city pages.
  • Companies registered — this already exists in the event payload and is currently rendered nowhere. It is the strongest event-specific proof on the page. Worth switching on.
  • Roles posted at the event — if you can feed it, the audience block uses it. If not, omit the block rather than estimating.
  • Show rate and per-employer averages are type-level, not per-event, and differ by type.
  • Every primary CTA carries eventId and a returnTo back to the page. On the current live page two imported links drop the eventId and fall back to the generic cart; that is a bug worth not reproducing.

The tutorial video

  • File: 1:04, 1920×1080, H.264, 3.3 MB, AAC stereo at −14.9 LUFS. Poster is 2560×1440.
  • Captions are burned into the picture. Do not upload a caption file — a caption track on top would show two sets of text to anyone who turns CC on. The transcript goes in the description, which is what YouTube indexes.
  • The handoff page linked at the top carries the YouTube title, the description to paste as-is, and the full transcript.
  • To move from the local file to YouTube: upload it, then replace the <video> string in the builder's TUT_SCRIPT with the iframe embed. One line.

Checking your work

  • Both pages must stay clear of horizontal overflow at a true 390px. Headless Chrome clamps its window to 500px, so probe inside an iframe or you are certifying the wrong width.
  • The prototypes use the live site's compiled Tailwind build. There is no runtime, so a class the live site never emitted silently does nothing. Anything new belongs in a scoped <style> block, which is the pattern every existing mock already follows.
  • Product mocks use the product blue #0044B3; page CTAs and eyebrows use the marketing blue #2563eb. They are not interchangeable.
