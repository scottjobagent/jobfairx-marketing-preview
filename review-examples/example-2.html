Example written review

This is what a good written review and photo look like.

How to write yours

  • Start with your first name and city.
  • In your own words, describe your experience interviewing through JobFairX, from scheduling to the interview itself.

How to take your photo

  • Smile, and frame it from the shoulders up.
  • Face a window or a light, so your face is bright and the light isn't behind you.
  • Use a plain background, with your phone at eye level.

When you are done, reply to our email with your review and photo.
