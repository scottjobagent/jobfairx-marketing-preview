Register & post jobs

Post Your Roles And Let Matching Fill The Schedule.

Register for an event in your market and post the jobs you're hiring for. Matching activates the moment the jobs are live, and the first interview requests arrive within a few hours.

  • Candidates are matched to what the role actually needs, not to a keyword count.
  • Every one is verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city.
  • Jobs are part of the event package — 1 on Starter, up to 3 on Growth, up to 6 on Pro.
  • Reuse a previous post as a template instead of starting over.

Candidate pipeline

They Ask For The Interview. You Just Say Yes.

Your pipeline isn't a stack of résumés to dig through — it's a list of matched candidates who have already asked for time with you, in five states, with the action on the row.

  • Requested, Scheduled, Interviewed, No-show and Declined — every candidate in exactly one.
  • View resume and Message sit on every row, in every state.
  • Auto-accept, or review each request — both in every package, at every tier.
  • Résumés and contact info for every matched candidate — not only the ones you met.

Interview scheduling

One Click Accepts. The Same Click Schedules It.

The request already carries a time. Accept isn't the first stage of an approval flow — it is the booking. The row moves to Scheduled at that exact slot, and the candidate is told.

  • Reschedule proposes a new time; until they confirm it reads “Proposed · awaiting candidate”.
  • Accepted requests land on one schedule, whichever event they belong to.
  • Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event, in every package.
See scheduling

Interview locations

Same Event. Your Choice Of Room.

Every employer joins the same event — one city, one date, one audience. What you set is where your own interviews happen, and candidates see it before they ever request a time. Two employers at one event can interview completely differently.

  • JobFairX video — runs inside the platform, nothing to install.
  • Phone — you call each candidate at the time you accepted.
  • In person — your street address, on their confirmation and every reminder.
  • Your own link — Teams, Zoom or Meet, if you're already standardised on one.
See interview locations

Event day & reporting

Run The Day From One Lobby, Then Get The Report.

On event day the lobby shows who is waiting, who is in a room, and who you are due to see next — whichever interview location you chose. The post-event report is ready the moment the event ends.

  • Dispositions, recruiter notes and analytics against every candidate.
  • Scheduled versus completed, attendance rate and missed interviews, per event.
  • A downloadable report with interview records and résumés, yours to keep.
