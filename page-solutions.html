Two axes, not one

The type is the audience. The interview location is yours.

An event is a market moment — the Dallas Healthcare Hiring Event, on its date, at its time — and every employer registered joins that same one. Where your interviews happen is a different question entirely, and you answer it yourself, per event.

Two employers at the same event can interview completely differently. One runs the JobFairX video call, another has candidates come to the office, a third picks up the phone, a fourth pastes in their own Teams link. In person is one of four interview locations — it is a setting on your row, never a kind of event, and an event itself carries no such thing. Because candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, meeting in person is a local ask rather than a trip. How interview locations work →

Use case, not a type

High-volume hiring. The answer is bundles.

There is no sixth type for hiring at volume. Filling dozens of roles means more events — more cities, more dates, or a bigger package at the ones you already attend — so the lever is the bundle, not a different kind of event.

  • Per event, Pro is the lever — 100+ scheduled interviews, up to 6 jobs and unlimited recruiter seats at $1,495, against 20+ interviews and 1 job on Starter at $495.
  • Across events, bundles are the lever — eight sizes from 5 to 100 events, reaching 40% off at the top of the ladder.
  • Credits never expire and are used across any event type, so one bundle covers a healthcare event in Dallas and a technology event in Chicago.
  • Registering 31 or more days ahead takes a flat $100 off any tier, on top of the bundle rate.
