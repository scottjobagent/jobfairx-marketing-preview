Four things these Terms do not settle

The audit recorded that no refund or cancellation policy exists anywhere on the site. It does — section 5 above, unchanged since 2021. The real problem is narrower and worse: the policy predates the product it governs, and four specific questions a paying employer will ask have no answer in this document. Each gap below is stated with the evidence behind it. Nothing here is invented, and nothing here is binding.

1. The cancellation window closes before registration does

Section 5 requires a cancellation at least 30 days before the event. But registration stays open until 1–2 days before it, and the countdown banners actively sell into that window. An employer who registers 20 days out has, from the moment they pay, no way to cancel: section 5 routes them straight into the no-show policy, where the fee is forfeited and cannot be rolled over.

  • Terms §5: cancellations “must be received a minimum of 30 days prior to the event”
  • Live site: early-registration pricing ends at exactly 31 days out (minDays: 31)
  • Live site: registration closes between 1 and 2 days before the event
  • Live banner, state C: “Candidate matching is live. Reserve your spot…”
  • Live banner, state E: “Employer registration closes tomorrow. Reserve your spot now →”

{{PLACEHOLDER — REFUND POLICY: what happens when an employer registers inside the 30-day window? Is there a cooling-off period, a shorter deadline for late registrations, or is the fee genuinely non-cancellable from purchase?}}

2. Bundle credits and cancellation credits are not the same thing — or are they?

Bundles run from 5 to 100 events, $470 down to $297 each, up to $29,700 at 100 events, and are sold under a “Credits never expire” trust chip. Section 5 separately promises “a credit … on their account for a future event” when you cancel. Whether those are one credit type or two, whether the never-expire promise covers a cancellation credit, and what becomes of an unused bundle balance are all unaddressed.

  • Bundles page: 5–100 events, $470 → $297 per event, $29,700 at 100
  • Bundles page trust chips: “Credits never expire” · “Use across any event type”
  • Terms §5: “a credit will be issued on their account for a future event”
  • Terms §5: no-shows “are not eligible to roll the registration package over”

{{PLACEHOLDER — BUNDLE CREDIT TERMS: do bundle credits and cancellation credits behave identically? Does “never expire” survive a cancellation, and what happens to an unused balance if an account closes?}}

3. Nothing covers JobFairX cancelling an event

Section 5 handles a date change — no refund, a credit instead. It says nothing about an event JobFairX cancels outright, an event that does not run, or an event that runs with too few candidates matched to be worth attending. Every obligation in the clause runs one direction.

  • Terms §5, Date Change: covers a moved event only
  • Terms §5: silent on cancellation by JobFairX
  • Live site: per-event candidate counts vary widely by market (e.g. 429, 728, 968 pre-registered)

{{PLACEHOLDER — EVENT CANCELLATION BY JOBFAIRX: what does an employer receive if we cancel an event, fail to run one, or run one materially below the interview volume their plan states?}}

4. The document predates the product it governs

These Terms took effect on November 01, 2021 and still speak the language of a physical trade show: no-shows forfeit their registration fee “and materials”. There are no materials. There is also nothing about interview locations — the employer who sets an in-person interview location and then cannot host, the employer whose own video link fails, or the candidate who does not attend a confirmed interview.

  • Terms: “Effective as of November 01, 2021”
  • Terms §5: “forfeit their entire registration fee and materials”
  • Terms: no reference to interview locations, video, phone or in person
  • Product: interview location is set per event — JobFairX video, phone, in person, or your own link
