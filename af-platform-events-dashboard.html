Platform — Events dashboard

Four events. Four interview locations. One screen.

The events dashboard is one row per event you have registered for. Each row carries the interview location you set on that event — JobFairX video, phone, in person at your address, or your own link — what is still incomplete, how many seats and jobs your package gives you, and how many candidates are pending or upcoming.

The real screen

One row per event, and everything that row owes you.

This is the Events tab, unretouched. Hover a pin — or a caption — to see which part of the screen it belongs to.

How a row happens

Register. Set up. Run it.

01 — Register

The event is the moment

Register for an event and a row appears. The event is a market moment — Dallas Healthcare, Apr 22, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM CT — and every employer attending joins that same one.

02 — Set up

You choose your room

You set your own interview location and add your jobs. Two employers at the same event can interview completely differently, and the row shows which of the two steps you still owe.

03 — Run it

From the same row

Go to lobby sits on the row itself, so the screen you registered from is the screen you run the event day from.

Setup state

Nothing is half-configured on event day without saying so.

Setup state is a property of the row. Two of these events carry a blue Complete setup button and a red Setup chip; two carry “Go to lobby ● Setup complete”. You do not have to open an event to find out which one it is.

  • The red Incomplete appears in the column that is actually missing something — Interview setup on one row, Job titles on another.
  • “0 of 1 jobs · Add a job” — the fix is a link on the row, not a support ticket.
  • Go to lobby is on every row, finished or not, so testing the room is never blocked.
  • A link at the top of the table offers to test your audio and video before your event.
  • Dedicated human support is included in every package, for the rows that still do not explain themselves.

Seats and jobs

Metered on the row, and the meter is your package.

Every row shows what you have used against what you bought: 1 of 5 seats, 4 of unlimited seats, 1 of 1 seat; 1 of 3 jobs, 3 of 6 jobs, 0 of 1 jobs. Pricing stops being an abstraction the moment it is a fraction on a row.

Three packages, one meter

  • Starter · $495 — promote 1 job, 2 recruiter seats, 20+ scheduled interviews.
  • Growth · $895 — up to 3 jobs, up to 5 recruiter seats, 60+ scheduled interviews.
  • Pro · $1,495 — up to 6 jobs, unlimited recruiters, 100+ scheduled interviews.
  • Prices are per event, and unlimited interviews before, during and after the event are included at every tier. See pricing.

Job postings

Your job postings live inside the event.

Job titles is a column on the events dashboard because the jobs belong to the event you registered for. There is no separate job-postings product to buy, and no second place to keep them in sync.

The meter follows you in

  • The same meter follows you into the post itself — “Growth package · 2 of 3 jobs added” sits under the heading while you write it.
  • Promote 1 job on Starter, up to 3 on Growth, up to 6 on Pro.
  • Each posted title is a link on the row, so the jobs and the event never drift apart.
  • AI matching and job promotion is the first item on the All Packages Include list — matching activates as soon as your jobs are posted.

One account

One account, however many events you run.

Upcoming events and Past events are two tabs on the same table, and “Register for an event” adds the next row from here. Teams that hire in more than one city are not running more than one login.

  • Buy events in bundles from 5 to 100 — $470 per event down to $297 — with credits that never expire and can be used across any event type.
  • Register 31 or more days ahead and early registration takes a flat $100 off any tier.
  • 4,000+ employers have run events across 300+ cities, and 87% of them come back.
  • Past events stay in the account — follow-up interviews and messaging continue after the event, and the post-event report arrives with downloadable data.
