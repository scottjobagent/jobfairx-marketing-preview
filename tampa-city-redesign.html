Build notes

For Brandon. The Tampa city front door page, redesigned to match the Tampa Technology page.

What this is

The Tampa city front door page (/job-fairs-near-me/florida/tampa) in the same design system as the Tampa Technology page redesign. The shared pieces (container, spacing, fonts, colours, buttons, the name grid, job cards, category cards, the market list, nearby cards and the How it works timeline) are described in that page's build notes. Every word, heading, link and all 11 sections match the live page captured on 27 Sep 2026. The header and footer are the live ones.

  • The job feed and dates refresh daily; the markup is what matters.
  • The first FAQ answer is shown open for review only. On the page, every answer starts closed.

Page layout

  • Same as the Technology page: full-width sections, one container (max-width 1280px with 32px sides, so 1216px of content; 24px sides under 1024px), 90px above and below each section (48px under 1024px), Inter headings at the live sizes, blue capital section labels, 16px corners.
  • Backgrounds: Hero (navy) → Upcoming events and For employers (white) → Past employers (light grey) → Jobs in Tampa right now (white) → Job fair types (light grey) → Local job market (white) → FAQ (light grey) → Nearby (white) → How it works (light grey) → Reviews (white) → footer. Light grey is #F8FAFC with 1px #E2E8F0 lines above and below.

Pieces new on this page

  • Hero. A navy band. The H1 and intro sit on the left, with the "This week" line under them in the Technology page's status box; "View this event" is a light-blue underlined link. "Are you here to find a job, or to hire?" becomes a white card on the right (420px, 16px radius): the question at 17px, weight 600, then "I'm looking for a job" as the brand-blue primary pill and "I'm hiring in Tampa" as a white pill with a slate border, both full width with an arrow that nudges right on hover. The HTML order is unchanged (H1, chooser, status line); CSS grid places the chooser on the right. Under 1024px everything stacks.
  • Upcoming events. One white list (1px #E2E8F0 border, 16px radius) with a row per event: the date (15px, weight 600) and time (13px, grey) in an 11rem column; "Tampa, FL Job Fair" (15px, weight 600) with the formats line (13px, grey); then the category tag and "Learn more" with an arrow in brand blue. Tags use the live colours: Technology indigo, Entry-Level sky, Veteran rose, Healthcare teal, Diversity amber (50 background, 700 text, 200 ring). Row hover: #F8FAFC, and the arrow nudges right. On phones each row stacks as on the live page (title and tag, then the date, then a full-width "View event details" button); the live page's duplicate tag and link spans are kept and shown per screen size (.d-only, .m-only). "View all Florida job fairs" sits right-aligned under the list (left-aligned on phones).
  • For employers. A navy band with a 16px radius and the Register band's soft blue glow in its top-right corner. On the left: the label in light blue, the H2 at 28px (22px on phones), the three facts as a list with light-blue dots, then a thin line with Target, Allstate and Trinity Health in a row under it. On the right, one above the other: "See Tampa hiring event pricing" as the white primary button (the rule for buttons on navy) and "Talk to our team" as an outlined white pill. Under 1024px the buttons move under the text and run the band's full width. The band follows the events list with no top padding, as on the live page.
  • Jobs in Tampa right now. The Technology page's job cards, two across on desktop, grouped as on the live page: each group starts with its category tag (with the category's dot) and the count ("4 jobs") in grey.
  • Job fair types. The Technology page's Other job fairs cards (the live design), five across on desktop.
  • Nearby. The Technology page's city cards, four across in two rows, on a white section without the dot pattern. "All 24 Florida cities with job fairs →" sits centred under them (left-aligned on phones).
  • Reviews. Wayne M.'s video card on the left (360px wide, a 4:5 frame over the 720 × 1280 poster, a white "Video · 0:17" pill with a navy play circle; name, role and date under it). The two written reviews stack on the right (the quote at 16px, a 44px round photo, the name, place and role, and the date). The disclaimer line sits under them at 12px. Clicking the play pill starts the video and shows the browser's own controls. Under 1024px the video card sits above the two reviews.

Fix carried over from the live page

On the live page the FAQ answers are only in the page's data and appear after a click, so Google doesn't read them. This design puts them in the page with <details> and <summary>, using the live answer text.
