Security and trust

What we can evidence, and what we can’t yet.

This page is written to survive a vendor review rather than to close one. Every claim below is paired with the evidence behind it. Where the evidence does not exist, the card says so, names what is missing and leaves a blank for the answer — including our certifications, which we do not hold.

4,000+ employers have run events with us and 500,000+ interviews have been conducted on the platform. None of that is a security control, and we are not going to present it as one.

0 certifications held

Read this before you read anything else

JobFairX holds no third-party security certification today. Not SOC 2, not ISO 27001. There is no report to send you, and nothing here is “in progress” in a way we can evidence.

If your review requires a completed SOC 2 Type II report, we will not pass it this quarter. If it requires straight answers, a named owner and signed paperwork, we can do that this week. A page that says “SOC 2 Type II — not yet certified, targeted {{DATE}}” is worth more in a review than one that implies otherwise and gets found out at the questionnaire stage.

01 — Data handling and retention

What we hold, where it came from, how long it stays

Two of these have public answers today. The rest are blanks, and the blank is the honest answer until the policy is written.

Candidates come from our own audience, not from scraping

Evidenced

JobFairX maintains a database of over 3 million job seekers actively looking for new opportunities, added through targeted digital marketing, social media advertising, job distribution across major platforms, email campaigns and organic search. They register with us; they are not lifted from somewhere else.

The field-level map of what a candidate submits, and which fields are optional, is {{CANDIDATE DATA MAP}}.

Evidence Employer FAQ — “How are candidates sourced?”, published on jobfairx.com.

What leaves our system and enters yours

Evidenced

Every package includes résumés and contact information for every matched candidate, plus a post-event report with downloadable data. That is personal data crossing into your systems — from that moment your own retention and deletion rules govern it, not ours.

Evidence Pricing page — “All Packages Include”, items 2 and 6.

05 — Candidate data and EEOC posture

The inversion changes the compliance questions

Candidates request interviews; you accept, and accepting schedules them. Nobody is pushed into your pipeline. That helps with some questions and creates others — both are below.

Candidates initiate, and they opt in

Evidenced

Matched job seekers request an interview with you. You review the résumé and accept or decline; accepting schedules the interview. Everyone in your pipeline asked to be there.

Evidence Employer FAQ — “How does candidate matching and interviewing work?”

Candidates are local to the event city

Evidenced

Candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city. The method behind that verification — self-declared, inferred, or documented — is {{VERIFICATION METHOD}}, and a reviewer will ask, because location is inferred data.

Evidence Employer FAQ — “Are candidates local to the city where the event is held?”

What matching actually uses

Undocumented

Published: “job seekers whose background and interests align with your roles are invited to request interviews.” Which signals the model uses — and specifically whether any protected characteristic is among them — is {{MATCHING SIGNALS}}. This is the first question a legal reviewer asks, and it is the one we currently cannot answer.

Evidence Employer FAQ. The signal list itself is not published.

Audience-themed events

Undocumented

Events are themed by audience — Healthcare, Technology, Entry-Level, Diversity, Veteran. Our understanding is that a theme describes who an event is marketed to and who chooses to register, not a filter applied to matching. Whether any protected characteristic is used to include or exclude a candidate is {{UNANSWERED}}. The answer is probably benign. It still has to exist in writing before it is asked in a review.

Evidence Employer FAQ — “What types of targeted hiring events do you host?” Nothing states how a theme interacts with matching.

Automated decisions and AI hiring law

Undocumented

Automatic interview scheduling accepts requests on your behalf. It books a conversation; we have no evidence it rejects, ranks or scores anyone — but “no evidence” is not a compliance position. Three questions need answering before an enterprise legal team asks them:

  • Does matching rank or score candidates, or only surface them? {{UNCONFIRMED}}
  • Is matching an automated employment decision tool under NYC Local Law 144, and has a bias audit been conducted? {{NOT CONDUCTED — CONFIRM}}
  • Illinois AI Video Interview Act — is any notice or consent required for JobFairX video interviews? {{UNANSWERED}}

Evidence Employer FAQ — “You can also enable automatic interview scheduling to accept requests automatically.” Pricing page — “auto-accept or review each request”.

06 — Availability during live events

An outage inside the window is not degraded service. It is a candidate who took the morning off.

An event is a fixed window, and interviews inside it are half-hour slots with a person waiting on each end. There is no retry at 11:15. That makes availability the security question with the shortest fuse for this product, and it is where a generic uptime page would be most misleading.

Support window

Evidenced

Mon–Fri, 5:00 AM – 5:00 PM PST. info@jobfairx.com and (702) 269-0808. Dedicated human support is included in every package, at every tier — it is not an enterprise add-on.

Evidence Contact page hours; pricing page — “All Packages Include”, item 8.

Evidence Published support hours are weekdays only. Event start times vary by market and time zone; the overlap has not been checked.

Incident and breach notification

Undocumented

Who tells you, how quickly, and through which channel — during a live event and after a data incident: {{INCIDENT COMMS}} {{BREACH NOTIFICATION TIMELINE}}. Most enterprise contracts require 72 hours in writing.

Evidence None on file.

Three of the four interview locations survive an outage of ours

Evidenced

You set your own interview location per event. If it is phone, in person at your address, or your own Zoom, Teams or Meet link, the conversation does not run on our infrastructure at all — you have the time slot and you have your own room. Only JobFairX video puts the interview inside our platform. That is architecture, not a promise, and it is the strongest availability answer we own.

Evidence Product — the interview location column on the events dashboard: JobFairX video call, you call each candidate, a street address, or your own link.

A lost slot can be rebooked

Evidenced

Every package includes unlimited interviews before, during and after the event, and employer access doesn’t expire — you can keep scheduling follow-ups for as long as you need. A slot lost to a failure can be rebooked at no extra cost. What cannot be restored is the candidate’s willingness to come back, and that is the real cost of an incident here.

Evidence Pricing page — “All Packages Include”, item 3; employer FAQ — “What happens after the hiring event?”
