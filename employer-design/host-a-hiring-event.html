How to host a hiring event that ends in hires

Hosting your own job fair means a venue, promotion, screening, scheduling and follow-up. Here is the checklist, and the version where JobFairX does the heavy lifting.

Short answer: A hiring event works when it is built around scheduled interviews, not foot traffic. Decide the roles, pick a format (in person, video or phone), get the right candidates registered, schedule the interviews before the day, run the day from one place and follow up within 48 hours. JobFairX hosts hiring events in 300+ cities that do all of that: you post jobs, matched candidates request interviews, and you meet them on the day.

Decide what the event is for

Start with the hires, not the event. Which roles, how many openings, and who on your team will interview. A hiring event for one role runs differently from one for six: fewer interviewers, a shorter time block, and a tighter screening question.

  • List the job titles you will interview for, with the pay you will publish.
  • Decide who interviews. Hiring managers who can say yes on the day shorten everything that follows.
  • Set the number you need to interview to make one hire. If you do not know, use your last ten hires as the guide.

Choose the format: in person, video or phone

An in-person event needs a room, parking and a greeter. A video event needs working links and a quiet place for each interviewer. A phone event needs nothing but a schedule. Pick the one your candidates will actually show up for, which for hourly and local roles is usually in person or phone, and for salaried roles increasingly video.

On JobFairX you select the interview location when you set up your jobs: in person at your address, on JobFairX video with nothing to install, or by phone. It never changes the price. See in-person, video or phone interviews.

Pick the date and the time block

A four-hour block is enough for most teams. The question is how long each interview runs: interview slots are 15 or 30 minutes. A standard 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM event offers 16 start times at 15 minutes or 8 at 30 minutes, and your team decides how many interviews run in each one.

  • Give yourself at least one week between announcing the event and holding it, so candidates can register and interviews can be scheduled.
  • Avoid the first and last hour of the working day for in-person events; attendance drops at both ends.
  • Put a buffer after every third interview. Interviews run long when they are going well.

Get the right candidates registered

If you host it yourself, this is the expensive part: job boards, social posts, email to past applicants, flyers at schools and workforce centres, and a registration form so you know who is coming. Walk-in traffic is unpredictable and unscreened.

JobFairX events start with candidates already registered. JobFairX has a database of more than 3 million job seekers, with over 2,200 new registrations each day. Candidates register for specific events by city and date, and are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city. Once you post your jobs, your roles are matched with registered candidates and qualified matches are invited to request an interview. See the local candidate database.

Screen and schedule before the day

The difference between a job fair and a hiring event is the schedule. Screen first, then put every interview on a timetable with a name attached. Candidates who hold a time show up; candidates told to "stop by" often do not.

  • Write two or three screening questions that a candidate can answer in a minute: licence held, shift availability, distance.
  • Confirm every interview in writing, then remind the day before and the morning of.
  • Keep a short waitlist for each interviewer so a no-show is filled.

On JobFairX, candidate matching activates immediately once you post your jobs and interview requests start within a few hours. You review candidate résumés before accepting or declining, or enable auto-accept to confirm interviews automatically. Candidates receive automated emails and SMS leading up to their scheduled interview. See interview requests.

Run the day from one place

Whoever runs the event needs one view of who is waiting, who is interviewing and who is done. On paper that is a clipboard and a runner; on JobFairX it is the event lobby, which shows who is waiting to interview, who is interviewing and who has been interviewed, with the schedule, résumés and notes in the same place. Panel interviews let more than one interviewer join the same video interview. See the event-day lobby.

Follow up within 48 hours

Most events lose their hires in the week after. Decide outcomes on the day (yes, no, maybe), send offers to the yeses within two days and tell the rest where they stand.

JobFairX sends a post-event report with recruiter notes, interview records and résumés, and your access does not expire: you can keep scheduling follow-up interviews and messaging candidates for as long as you need. Automations can send the follow-up messages for you, after a missed interview and after the interview. See the post-event report and automations.

FAQ

How much does it cost to host a hiring event?

If you host it yourself, the cost is the venue, promotion and your team's time, and it varies with all three. A JobFairX hiring event is a flat rate per event: Starter is $495 for 1 promoted job and 20+ scheduled candidate interviews, Growth is $895, Pro is $1,495.

How far in advance should I plan a hiring event?

Register at least one week in advance so candidate matching can begin and interview requests can start coming in. For an event you host yourself, allow longer for promotion.

Do I need a booth or table?

No. There is no venue, booth or table at a JobFairX event. You interview candidates in person at your address, on JobFairX video, or by phone.

Can I host a virtual hiring event?

Yes. Video interviews run directly inside JobFairX, with no Zoom, no downloads and no external software required. See virtual hiring events.
