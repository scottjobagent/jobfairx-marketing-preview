Regardless of location

What does not change

Interview location is the last decision, not the first one. Everything that actually fills your schedule works identically in all four.

Same matching

Post your open jobs and candidate matching activates immediately — you start receiving interview requests within a few hours.

Same interview requests

Matched candidates request the interview, in all four. The direction of travel never reverses — you are not the one chasing anybody for a callback.

Same one-click accept

Review the résumé, accept, and the interview is scheduled. Or turn on automatic interview scheduling and accept every request without touching it.

Same schedule

One event window, one schedule, one set of reminders to your candidates. The location changes what the reminder says, not when it lands.

Same post-event report

Recruiter notes, interview records and résumés. Your access does not expire, and follow-up interviews and messaging stay open afterwards.

Same volume commitment

20+ scheduled interviews on Starter, 60+ on Growth, 100+ on Pro — tied to how many jobs you promote, never to where you meet people.

Read this before you assume

What this is not

Three things people read into "in person" that are not true here. They matter enough to say plainly rather than leave to inference.

JobFairX does not run in-person job fairs.

There is no venue, no floor plan, no booth and no table to rent. We run hiring events; what launched is the option to hold your own interviews in a room instead of on a call.

You are not hosting your own event.

You join the same Dallas Healthcare Hiring Event as every other employer registered for it — one date, one time window, one candidate audience. You are not producing an event, promoting one, or filling one.

In person is not an event type.

The five event types are Healthcare, Diversity, Veteran, Technology and Entry-Level — that axis describes the candidates. Interview location is a separate, per-employer setting on a separate axis entirely.

The location is only where your interviews happen. It has no effect on the event, on anyone else registered for it, or on which candidates you are matched to.
