Interview location

You choose where your interviews happen

Set it once when you register and every interview you accept follows it. This is your setting, not the event’s — another employer at the same event can interview a different way.

  • In person, at your address

    Add your parking and check-in notes. Candidates get them with their interview confirmation.

  • On JobFairX video

    Runs in the browser with nothing to install and no link to send. If your team already has its own video tool, you can use your own link instead.

  • By phone

    You call each candidate at the time you accepted.

Whichever you choose, it never changes the price.
