Build notes

For Brandon. Tampa Technology Job Fair page, redesigned in the ploy.ai direction.

What this is

A visual redesign of the city × vertical event page, shown on Tampa Technology (/job-fairs/florida/tampa/next-technology). It follows the look of ploy.ai in JobFairX colours. Only the visual layer changes. Every word, heading level, link, button and the section order match the live page as captured on 26 Sep 2026. The header, footer, title, meta description and structured data are unchanged.

  • The content is the live page's, captured 26 Sep 2026. The job feed refreshes during the day, so the ten jobs here won't match the live list later. The markup is the same.
  • The first FAQ answer is shown open for review only. On the page, every answer starts closed.

Fonts

  • Add Anton from Google Fonts (one weight, 400) for the H1 and the section H2s only.
  • Headings use the live site's sizes: H1 52px (30px on phones), section H2s 36px (26px), the Local job market H2 26px (22px).
  • Everything else stays Inter at the live site's size and weight. Every text style was audited against the live page on 26 Sep at 1440px and 375px.
  • Display type is set in capitals with CSS (text-transform: uppercase). The text in the HTML keeps its normal case.

Page rhythm

  • Sections alternate. Open sections sit on the grey page with a centred heading. Panel sections are rounded panels (24px radius) inset 24px from the window edge, 10px on phones, with content up to 1152px wide.
  • Order: Hero (navy panel) → Past employers (open) → Jobs and Register band (blue panel) → Other job fairs (open) → Local job market (white panel) → Preparation (open) → FAQ (blue panel) → Nearby (open) → How it works (navy panel) → footer.
  • 128px between sections on desktop, 80px on phones.

Shared pieces

  • Pill label (the eyebrow): 28px tall, navy with white text. On navy panels it turns white with navy text.
  • Section H2: Anton, navy, 36px (26px on phones), line-height 1.08, text-wrap: balance.
  • Hairline panels (Past employers, Other job fairs, Nearby): white, 1px #E2E8F0 border, 20px radius, cells split by 1px lines.
  • Quiet pills (View job, View events, the round arrows): white, 1px #CBD5E1 border, navy text. They fill navy when their row or card is hovered. The whole row or card stays the link.
  • Register free keeps the brand blue. On navy it adds a 1px light ring and a soft blue glow so it separates from the background.

Rules for other cities and verticals

  1. H1. 52px on desktop and 30px on phones, like the live site. Tampa Technology fits on one line; longer city names wrap to a second line.
  2. Headings with a Q. Anton's Q has a tail that reaches into the next line. Keep every Anton heading at line-height 1.02 or more (they're 1.02–1.12 here).
  3. Metro heading. The Local job market heading is 26px (22px on phones), line-height 1.12. Long metro names break at their hyphens.
  4. Event details card on phones. The label column is 5.6rem, so 11:00 AM–3:00 PM EDT stays on one line.
  5. Jobs footer. The feed note sits on the left and See all Tampa jobs on the right (flex-direction: row-reverse; the HTML order is unchanged). On phones they stack with the link first.
  6. Market figures. In the Local job market paragraph, each figure is wrapped in <span class="fig"> (bold navy). The paragraph's words are unchanged.

Fix carried over from the live page

On the live page the FAQ answers are added only after a question is clicked, so they aren't in the HTML Google reads. This design uses <details> and <summary>: all six answers are in the page from the start and fold closed.

Also noticed on the live page

Not part of this design. The meta description is built from job titles and mangles them: "Employers interviewing for senior principal product managers, dynatrace software engineerings and more." The same sentence is in the Event structured data.

Checks done on this file

  • Keyboard focus shows a 2px outline on every link and button (light blue on navy).
