About JobFairX

We point the hiring pipeline the other way.

JobFairX runs hiring events in 300+ cities. Employers post their open roles; matching puts those roles in front of candidates verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city; those candidates request interviews. You accept, and the interview is scheduled. No stack of applications ever forms, because nobody was asked to send one.

Candidates come to you already matched, already interested, and already asking for an interview — you just say yes.

How we think about hiring

Three ideas the product is built on.

Not a mission statement. These are three decisions you can see in the software, and most of what JobFairX does differently follows from one of them.

01

Candidates request. They don’t apply.

An application is a message sent into the dark, and it puts the burden of interest entirely on the person who sent it. A request is different: someone who has already seen your role is asking to meet you.

So the platform has no apply button for you to triage. Matched candidates request an interview, you accept or decline, and accepting is what schedules it. You can review a résumé first, or turn on automatic scheduling and skip that step.

02

The interview is the unit, not the application.

We do not count submissions, because a hiring team cannot do anything with a submission. Packages are sold in scheduled interviews — 20+ on Starter, 60+ on Growth, 100+ on Pro — since that is the thing that has to land on a calendar before anyone gets hired.

500,000+ interviews have been conducted on the platform. That figure, not a résumé count, is how we judge whether any of this is working.

03

The employer chooses the room.

An event is a market moment — a city, an audience, a date and a time. Every employer joins the same event. What each employer sets independently is their interview location: JobFairX video, phone, in person at their own address, or their own link.

Two employers at the same event can interview completely differently, and neither has to accommodate the other. Since candidates are already local to the event city, an address is a workable answer rather than a travel request.
