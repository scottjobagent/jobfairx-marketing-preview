Platform · Event day

Event day is a queue you work down.

The lobby shows every candidate who has turned up, how long each one has been waiting, and which of your rooms is free. Start interview sits on the row. Nothing about the four hours is left to improvise.

  1. 01 — Waiting to interview

    They turn up

    A candidate whose interview you accepted arrives at their slot and lands in the lobby. Their wait time starts counting on the row.

  2. 02 — Interviewing

    You start the room

    The row carries the name, the desired job, the desired location and the interview time. Status reads Ready, and Start interview opens the room.

  3. 03 — Interviewed

    The count moves

    When the call ends the candidate lands under Interviewed. That tab count is your running total for the day, visible without leaving the lobby.

The real screen

The lobby, live, two hours and forty-two minutes from the end.

This is the event-day lobby, unretouched, mid-event. Hover a pin — or a caption — to see which part of the screen it belongs to.

The lobby is one screen per event. Candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, so “desired location” on these rows is a local answer — Dallas, TX or Remote, not a national one.

Proof 01

Four tabs. Every candidate is on exactly one of them.

The counts across the top are the state of your event, and they move on their own as the day runs. You work the first tab; the other three are the score.

  1. 6 ON THIS SCREEN Waiting to interview Here now, wait time running. This tab splits into Interview rooms — ready to start — and Waiting rooms. ReadyEntering interview roomAwaiting available interviewer
  2. 3 ON THIS SCREEN Interviewing In session right now — three interviews running at the same time, across your recruiter seats.
  3. 5 ON THIS SCREEN Interviewed Finished. The count is your running total for the day, and it is the number the post-event report is built from.
  4. 26 ON THIS SCREEN Not yet interviewed Everyone else on your list for this event who has not been through a room yet.

Proof 02

Rooms run in parallel. Your seats decide how many.

The waiting tab is two tables, not one. Interview rooms holds the candidates a free interviewer can take right now. Waiting rooms holds the rest — and the status on those rows tells you which kind of waiting it is.

  • Entering interview room — moving through. Nothing for you to do.
  • Awaiting available interviewer — every seat is busy. That is the one that costs you a candidate, and the wait time beside it is how much it has cost so far.
  • Seats by tier: 2 recruiter seats on Starter, up to 5 on Growth, unlimited recruiters on Pro.
  • Your status: Available sits in the lobby header, so each recruiter says when they are free rather than being assumed to be.

Proof 03

Notes, résumé and chat sit beside the call.

Inside the room the candidate's name and the job they want are on screen, a timer runs from the moment it opens, and the tools you need during the conversation are on the same bar as mute and End. No second tab, no second app.

  • Notes — written in the room, and they come back out in the post-event report with your recruiter notes and interview records.
  • Resume — the same résumé that sits on the candidate's row, one click from the call.
  • Chat — in the room, with the person you are talking to.
  • Background, mic and video controls are the rest of the bar. This is the JobFairX video interview room — nothing for the candidate to download or install.

Proof 04

Phone or in person? Same queue. You just meet somewhere else.

The event is a market moment — Dallas Healthcare Hiring Event, Apr 22, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM CT — and every employer joins that same one. What you set is your interview location, and the lobby header carries it. On this screen it reads Video · JobFairX video call.

  • Four locations to choose from: JobFairX video, phone — you call each candidate — in person at your address, or your own link, which the dashboard names.
  • The four tabs, the two tables, the wait times and the statuses are the same screen whichever one you picked. The queue does not change shape because the room did.
  • Two employers at this same Dallas event can be running completely differently: one on JobFairX video, one with candidates walking into their office.
  • Candidates are verified within approximately 20 miles of the event city, so asking them to come to you is a local ask, not a national one.

Proof 05

At 3:00 the screen changes job.

When the countdown runs out the header reads Ended, the tabs become Summary, Interviewed and Not interviewed, and the page stops being a queue and starts being a handover.

  • Interviewing has ended — “You can schedule follow-up interviews, review feedback, and update notes in your completed event report.”
  • Follow up with 12 candidates you interviewed, with a View Interview Summary link straight to the feedback and notes.
  • Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event are in every package, Starter through Pro — so the day ending is not the hiring ending.
  • Résumés and contact info for every matched candidate stay with you, and follow-up interviews and messaging after the event are on the same included list.
