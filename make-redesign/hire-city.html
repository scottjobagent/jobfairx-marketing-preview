What you choose

Interview Houston candidates in person, on video, or by phone.

Set the format, the time slot duration and panel interviews when you register. In person means at your Houston location.

Interview the way your team hires.

Choose in-person, video, or phone when you register. In person is at your own location, video is on JobFairX with nothing to install, and for phone you call the candidate.

Screen in 15 minutes or go deep in 30.

Pick the interview time slot duration for each event. Fifteen minutes suits screening candidates; thirty suits full interviews. How many interviews your team runs in each slot is a separate setting.

Bring your whole panel into one interview.

On video interviews, more than one interviewer can join the same interview. Turn it on for every interview on the event, or add interviewers to a single interview from its row.
