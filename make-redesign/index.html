5. Product accuracy checklist (applied on every page)

Stated as the product works

Format is chosen per event at registration: in person at the employer's own location, video on JobFairX, phone where the recruiter calls the candidate. Time slot duration is 15 or 30 minutes per event, framed as screening vs full interviews, never as capacity. Panel interviews are video on JobFairX only; the panel switch disables when In-Person or Phone is selected (click the formats in any hero panel). Candidates request interviews, employers accept or decline, and an interview is confirmed only after acceptance. Interviews run before, during and after the event.

Claims reused from the live site only

4,000+ employers, 500,000+ interviews, 300+ cities, 3M+ candidates; the organisation names in the logo strip; the three results cards and their quotes, verbatim; the five event types and their descriptions; $495 / $895 / $1,495 tiers, bullets and the eight "All packages include" items; 87% return, 91% satisfaction, $28 average cost per interview. No new metrics, endorsements, integrations, guarantees or AI claims were added.
