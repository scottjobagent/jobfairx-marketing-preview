Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Are candidates local to the city where the event is held?

JobFairX promotes the event to relevant candidates in the region and the wider area, while making the interview format and location clear before they request time with you.

How are candidates sourced?

JobFairX matches your posted roles to candidates who fit and promotes the opportunity directly to them. Candidates who are interested can request an interview with your team.

How are candidates prepared for interviews?

Candidates receive the role details, interview time, format, and attendance instructions before their scheduled conversation so they can arrive ready to engage.

Can my team follow up after the event?

Yes. Your post-event report lets your team review outcomes, access candidate details, send messages, and schedule next-round conversations.
