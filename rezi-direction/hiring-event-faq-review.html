Hiring event FAQ, rezi user-guides shape: build notes for Brandon

Design reference for jobfairx.com/employer/hiring-event-faq, built in the shape of rezi's User Guides page: a pill and a title, a Popular row of three picture cards, then a sidebar with a search box and the categories beside grouped question cards. Same tokens, nav and footer as the other rezi-direction pages, and a different shape from all of them. The ten questions and their answers are the live page's, read from its FAQPage JSON-LD on 23 Oct 2026; the title, meta description, the H1, the sub-line and the closing copy are the live ones. New strings are marked below.

Page map

  1. Header: the "FAQ" pill on a rule, the H1 "Frequently Asked Questions", the live line "Everything you need to know about our hiring events."
  2. Popular: three cards with a product capture, the question and the first sentence of its answer. They open the matching question below. The three picked here are how job fairs work, how many interviews to expect, and how to register. New copy: the line "Start with the questions employers ask first." Which three are popular is Scott's call (the live page does not rank them).
  3. Sidebar (sticky): "Search by keyword" (filters the cards by question and answer text, with a match count and an empty state), and the four categories with counts (a click scrolls to the group). New copy: the category names How it works, Candidates, Interviews and results, Registration, and the empty-state line.
  4. Question cards: each category is a heading and a two-up grid of cards (icon and question). A card opens in place and spreads across both columns to show the answer; the answers are the live text, untouched.
  5. Closing card: the live "Ready to Start Hiring?" and "Browse our calendar and reserve your employer spot today." with Register for an Event linking to the calendar. Then the footer.

What stays exactly as it is on the live page

  • Title, meta description, canonical, the FAQPage JSON-LD (keep it), the ten questions and answers in the live order within their groups, the closing copy, the footer.

Motion

Header and Popular cards rise on load; the closing card reveals on scroll; category links scroll smoothly. All off under prefers-reduced-motion.

Breakpoints

At 1100px and below the sidebar narrows. At 900px and below the Popular cards stack, the sidebar becomes a search box and a row of category chips above the questions, and the cards go one per row.

Decisions for Scott

  2. The four category names and how the ten questions are grouped.
  3. Cards that open in place (as built) versus the live accordion list.
  4. Keep the search box?
  5. The two new lines (Popular sub-line, empty state).
