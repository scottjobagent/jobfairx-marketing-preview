Employer event page, version 2 (rezi tool-page shape): build notes for Brandon

Design reference for the employer event-page template (Scottsdale Technology and the four other types behind the switch on the left). Version 1 (22 Oct) reused the home page's layout; Scott asked for one rezi layout per kind of page, so this version takes the shape of rezi's Resume Checker page (the hero does the job) and the pinned feature band from rezi's AI Resume Builder page. Same tokens, nav and footer as the other rezi-direction pages; every word is the live event page's copy and every number is live data. Version 1 is kept in v1-same-layout/.

Page map

  1. Hero, two columns with one vertical rule between them. Left: the event line (city and type pill, date and time), the H1 in one colour, the live sub-line, Reserve My Spot with the show-rate line beside it, and three checklist items (live copy from the home page). Right: the reservation card: event, date, time, interview formats, pre-registered candidates (data-dynamic="pre-registered-count"), the From price, a full-width Reserve My Spot, the registration countdown (data-dynamic="registration-countdown") and the averages line. This card replaces the stat strip, the product frame and the closing-band card of version 1.
  2. The difference: three cards with pictures. The home page's three points, each with a product capture. Then rezi's slim bar sitting on the rule: the candidate count and a small Reserve My Spot.
  3. How JobFairX works, dark band. The product frame stays pinned on the left while five steps scroll past on the right (Post jobs, AI matching, Review and confirm, Interview, Track results); each step carries its own capture into the frame and its checklist is the live page's bullet copy (candidate messaging, scheduling, tracking, the post-event report). On phones the frame is not pinned; each step shows its own picture above the text.
  4. Results: the type's heading and line, the past-company logos for that type, three employer quotes.
  5. Pricing: the three plans, All packages include, the bundles note. Unchanged content.
  6. FAQ: heading on the left, the six live questions on the right.
  7. Closing band: centered on navy, the countdown, the type's closing headline, the show-rate line, Reserve My Spot.
  8. Contact form and the footer, as on the live page.

The event-type switch (review aid, far left edge)

Unchanged from the first round: T / E / D / V / H switches the whole page between the five live event pages (Technology Scottsdale, Entry-Level Dallas, Diversity Birmingham, Veterans Dearborn, Healthcare Boise). Hash links #technology #entry-level #diversity #veteran #healthcare; the choice is remembered. Everything that changes per type carries a data-f hook, the past-company grid and the three quotes are rebuilt from the embedded JSON, and the title and meta description follow. Not part of the production page.

What stays exactly as it is on the live page

  • All copy, numbers, prices, the FAQ, the contact form and the footer. The Reserve buttons must keep the live checkout handler (data-live="reserve"); here they scroll to pricing.
  • Dynamic values are marked data-dynamic: the pre-registered count (hero card and slim bar) and the registration countdown (hero card and closing band). The averages and show rate come from the type's live page.
  • Keep the live page's Event JSON-LD, with the online-only attendance flag.

Motion

The hero rises in a stagger on load; sections reveal on scroll; the pinned frame cross-fades between captures as each step crosses the middle of the screen (IntersectionObserver with a 45% band). All off under prefers-reduced-motion.

Breakpoints

At 1100px and below the hero and the feature band go half and half; at 900px and below the hero stacks (card under the text, the vertical rule becomes a horizontal one), the cards stack, the pinned frame gives way to one picture per step, and the FAQ and contact grids stack.
