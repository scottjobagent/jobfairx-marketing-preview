Hiring event calendar, version 2 (rezi changelog and examples shape): build notes for Brandon

Design reference for jobfairx.com/employer/hiring-event-calendar. Version 1 (23 Oct, morning) reused the home page's opening block and closing band; Scott asked for one rezi layout per kind of page, so this version takes the shape of rezi's changelog (a plain title and one line, dated entries under big month headings) and its examples catalogue (a sticky sidebar with the search and the categories). Same tokens, nav and footer as the other rezi-direction pages. Every word is the live calendar's copy and every row is a real event read from the live page on 22 Oct 2026 (the first 100 it lists, October 27 to November 18). Version 1 is kept in v1-same-layout/.

Page map

  1. Header: the H1 "2026 Hiring events" as a plain small label, the H2 "Interview-Ready Candidates Matched to Your Jobs" as the title in one colour, the live sub-line, a hairline. No pill, no two-tone headline, no right column.
  2. Sidebar (sticky): the city search (the field shows "Search by city"; the live placeholder "Search by city (e.g. Dallas, TX)" is its tooltip and label, because the full text does not fit a 220px column: decision for Scott), the event types in the live order with the live colour per type and a count each, the months with a count each (a click jumps to that month), and the "Showing x of y" line. The counts follow the filters.
  3. List: one column-header row (Date, City, Type, Candidate matching), then each month as a big heading with its count and its hairline rows: date and weekday, "City, ST Hiring Event" linking to the live event page, type with its dot, the matching status pill (Live / Activates soon, data-dynamic="candidate-matching-status"), View Details. 30 rows, then Load More Events (+30). An empty state when nothing matches (new copy: "No events match. Try another city or type, or clear the filters.").
  4. Bundles card (live copy and link), then the footer. No closing band on this page.
  5. On phones the navy "Looking for a job?" strip sits under the nav as on the live phone layout; the sidebar becomes a block of chips above the list; rows stack with a chevron.

What stays exactly as it is on the live page

  • Title, meta description, canonical, the H1 and H2 texts, the sub-line, the two controls and their options, the four columns, the row content and links, Load More Events, the bundles card, the footer. Keep the live JSON-LD ItemList and the server-side filtering and paging; this file demonstrates the behaviour on the 100 rows it holds.

Motion

The header and sidebar rise on load, the first rows rise in a 40 ms stagger, the Live dot pulses, month jumps scroll smoothly. All off under prefers-reduced-motion.

Breakpoints

Above 1100px: sidebar 3/12, list 9/12. At 1100px and below the row columns tighten. At 900px and below the sidebar stops being sticky and becomes chips above the list, the column headers go, and rows stack.

  2. Date order with month groups (as built), or the live rotating order?
  3. 30 rows per page or the live 100?

Michael's SEO checks (23 Oct 2026)
