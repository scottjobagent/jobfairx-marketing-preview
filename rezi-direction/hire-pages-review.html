/hire sample: 10 cities, 60 pages

Built 23 October 2026 from the Employer city page (H) and Employer city x vertical (V) rows of Michael's tracker, which are marked Programmatic. Scott asked for a sample for Michael to approve, after which Brandon generates the rest. The ten cities are wave 1 cities with all five V rows and at least one event in the 22 October calendar snapshot, and none of them share an address with another city: Chicago, Colorado Springs, Dayton, Long Beach, Lubbock, Minneapolis, Albuquerque, Knoxville, San Jose and Tucson.

The city page takes rezi's templates shape: one jump card per event type, then one section per type. The city and type page takes rezi's tool shape, with the next event in a reservation card in the hero, because its job is the event page's job. Dates and statuses come from the calendar snapshot. Averages, audiences, logos and testimonials are the live type pages'. Values in brackets, such as candidates pre-registered and past results for the city, are data slots for the event data export on Scott's action list.
