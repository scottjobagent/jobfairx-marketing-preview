ZipRecruiter alternatives for employers: what to use instead, and when

ZipRecruiter sells distribution: one posting pushed to many boards, on a subscription. Here is what to use when distribution is not the problem.

Why employers look for a ZipRecruiter alternative

Third-party guides describe ZipRecruiter's model as subscription plans per job slot, quoted rather than published, with a short trial that converts to a paid plan and extra reach sold as an add-on. The common complaints follow from the model: the cost continues whether or not the role is filled, the applicants arrive unscreened, and the interviews are still yours to arrange. Confirm the current terms on ZipRecruiter's own site; sources below.

1. JobFairX hiring events

If the goal is interviews rather than distribution, a hiring event replaces the subscription with a flat rate per event. You post your jobs, candidate matching activates immediately, matched candidates request interviews and you confirm them, then interview in person at your address, on JobFairX video with nothing to install, or by phone. Packages: Starter $495 with 20+ scheduled candidate interviews, Growth $895 with 60+, Pro $1,495 with 100+. Candidates are local to the event city. See upcoming hiring events.

2. Indeed

The largest general board, with free posts within limits and sponsored jobs priced on a budget. Reach is the strength; screening remains yours. Indeed alternatives covers the detail.

3. LinkedIn Jobs

A free post with limits and promoted posts on a daily or total budget, per LinkedIn's pricing page. Better for salaried and remote roles than for hourly hiring.

4. Google's job listings

Free reach for any job on a careers page with job-posting structured data. Set this up before paying anyone for distribution.

5. Handshake

Campus recruiting with a reported free basic plan. For early-career roles; JobFairX's entry-level hiring events serve the same audience with interviews scheduled.

6. Niche boards

Smaller, better-fitting audiences for technology, healthcare, drivers and trades, sold per post or on contract.

7. Referrals and agencies

Referrals for fit, agencies for speed. Agree fees and guarantees in writing.

Your questions, answered

Is there a free alternative to ZipRecruiter?

Your careers page in Google's job listings, the free tiers on Indeed and LinkedIn within their limits, state job banks and social posts. Where to post jobs for free has the list.

Which is better for hourly roles, ZipRecruiter or a hiring event?

Hourly candidates respond to interviews on a date. A hiring event schedules them; a board posting asks them to apply and wait.

What does a JobFairX hiring event cost?

A flat rate per event: Starter $495, Growth $895, Pro $1,495, each with scheduled candidate interviews, résumés and contact details for matched candidates and a post-event report.
