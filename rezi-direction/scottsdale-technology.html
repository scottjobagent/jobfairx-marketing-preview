How JobFairX works

Hiring events built around interviews

Every interview on your schedule comes from a candidate who matched with your role and requested time with your team.

01 · Post jobs

Post your jobs and configure interview preferences.

Set your screening questions and interview settings: in person at your address, on JobFairX video, or by phone, with 15 or 30 minute interview slots.

02 · AI matching

AI matches registered candidates to your open positions.

Once you post your open jobs, candidate matching activates immediately and you will start receiving interview requests within a few hours.

  • Candidates verified within approximately 20 miles of the event city
  • A notification for each request and a summary email every morning at 9:00 AM
03 · Review & confirm

Review interview requests and confirm interviews.

Matched candidates request interview times. Accept requests individually, or enable auto-accept to confirm interviews automatically. You can review candidate résumés before accepting or declining.

  • Reach out before the event
  • Answer candidate questions
  • Keep candidates engaged before interviews
04 · Interview

Interview in person, video, or phone.

Meet candidates in person at your address, on JobFairX video with nothing to install, or by phone. Whatever you choose, the schedule, resumes, and notes are right there in the same place.

  • Suggest multiple interview times
  • Reschedule interviews with one click
  • Panel interviews let more than one interviewer join the same interview
05 · Track results

Interview candidates and track results.

Track which team member interviewed each candidate, review yes, no, or maybe outcomes, and identify no-shows.

  • See who has confirmed interviews
  • Follow up with pending candidates
  • Schedule follow-up interviews after the event
  • Post-event report with interview stats, candidate dispositions, and downloadable data

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about hiring events.

View all FAQs →

Do I need a booth or table?
No. There is no venue, booth, or table. You interview candidates in person at your address, on JobFairX video, or by phone.
How does candidate matching and interviewing work?
Once you post your open jobs, candidate matching activates immediately and you will start receiving interview requests within a few hours. You’ll receive a notification for each request and a summary email every morning at 9:00 AM. You can review candidate résumés before accepting or declining.
How many interviews can I expect?
It depends on your plan. Starter employers (1 job per event) average around 20 interviews. Growth employers (up to 3 jobs) average around 60. Pro employers (up to 6 jobs) typically see 100+.
Are candidates local to the city where the event is held?
Yes, candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, so you’re connecting with local, relevant talent.
How are candidates sourced?
JobFairX has a database of more than 3 million job seekers, with over 2,200 new registrations each day. Candidates register for specific events by city and date. Once you post your jobs, we match your roles with registered candidates and invite qualified matches to request an interview.
What happens after I register?
You receive a welcome message from JobFairX with a link to your dashboard. There you post your jobs and set your interview settings. Candidate matching is activated automatically.
