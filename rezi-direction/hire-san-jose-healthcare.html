How it works

Hiring events built around interviews

01Set up

Register for an event and post your jobs. Set your screening questions and interview settings. Candidate matching activates automatically once your jobs are posted.

02Review & confirm

Matched candidates request interview times. Accept requests individually, or enable auto-accept to confirm interviews automatically.

03Event day

Meet candidates in person at your address, on JobFairX video with nothing to install, or by phone. Whatever you choose, the schedule, resumes, and notes are right there in the same place.

04Post-event

Track which team member interviewed each candidate, review yes, no, or maybe outcomes, and identify no-shows.
