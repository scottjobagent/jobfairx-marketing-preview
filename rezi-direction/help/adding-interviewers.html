Adding interviewers

How to bring colleagues onto an event so they can interview candidates with you, how many you get, what lands in their inbox, and what they can do once they are in.

What an interviewer is

An interviewer is someone on your team who can interview candidates at one of your hiring events. You add them by email address, on the event you want them to work.

There are no permission levels

JobFairX has no roles, tiers or access settings to choose between. You add an email address and that person becomes an interviewer on that event. Everyone you add gets the same access, so add the people you want interviewing and nobody else. What that access covers is set out in What they can do.

Where to add them

Three places open the same Interview settings panel. Use whichever you are already looking at.

  1. From your dashboard In the Interview setup column, select the interviewer count on the event's row. Hovering it shows “Everyone interviewing at this event. Click to manage.” If the event has nobody on it yet, the column reads Incomplete with an Add link instead of a count.
  2. From the event's edit page Select the pencil on the event's row to open Edit your Jobs & Settings, then the pencil on the Interview settings card at the bottom. Its summary line tells you where you stand, for example “1 interviewer added | 1 daily update recipient”.
  3. From the lobby Open the event's lobby and look under Prepare for your interview. The Add interviewers to the event row has an Add interviewers button.

Adding someone

However you opened it, the panel is the same. Under Who will be interviewing candidates? is a box holding one chip per person, and your own address is marked (you).

  1. Select Add email… and type the person's email address.
  2. Press Enter. The address becomes a chip, and the count beneath the box goes up by one.
  3. Repeat for anyone else you want on the event.
  4. Select Update.

A green confirmation appears when it saves. From the edit page it reads “Changes saved”; from the dashboard it reads “Interview settings updated”. The panel then closes and its summary line updates.

Nothing saves until you select Update

Adding a chip changes only what is on screen. If you close the panel, or select Cancel, the person is not added. Two other things worth knowing: the count on your dashboard row can lag behind until you reload the page, and after removing someone the row may briefly show the event as Incomplete even though nothing is wrong. Reloading shows the true state.

How many interviewers you get

How many people you can put on an event depends on your package. Your own account is one of them.

The line under the email box tracks this as you go, and your dashboard row shows the same figure under the interviewer count. On Pro there is no ceiling, so the dashboard reads Unlimited in place of a number.

If you have used them all and need someone else on the event, remove an interviewer to free one up.

Update recipients are not interviewers

The same panel has a second field, Who needs to receive updates?, and it does something different. Addresses you put there are kept informed about the event but are not given access to interview. They receive:

  • an email each time a candidate requests an interview, and
  • a daily summary.

This field is required, so there is always at least one address on it. Use it for a hiring manager or coordinator who wants to follow the event without running interviews. Someone who needs to do both goes in both fields.

What the interviewer receives

As soon as you select Update, JobFairX emails everyone you added. The message comes from no-reply@jobfairx.com with the subject “You've been added as an interviewer”, and it carries:

  • the event name, its date, and its start and end time;
  • how the interviews will happen, for example Video and JobFairX video call;
  • the line “Log in with [their address] to view your interview schedule.”; and
  • a Go to interview lobby button.
Tell them to expect it

Nothing in the platform tells you the email has gone, and there is no pending or accepted state to watch. If someone says they have not received it, check the address on the chip for a typo, then have them look in their spam folder for a message from no-reply@jobfairx.com.

What an interviewer can see and do

Following the button in the email drops them straight into that event's lobby. Before the event it reads “Interviewing has not begun”, with “Candidates will appear here once they start the interview and are waiting for you to join.” Underneath is a Prepare for your interview panel with Review your candidates, Add interviewers and Set up automations.

All JobFairX virtual interviews at the top left takes them to their own events list. It shows only the events they have been added to, each marked Invited as interviewer, and they get the same left navigation you do.

Because there are no permission levels, an interviewer can do almost everything you can on that event, including editing it, adding jobs, adding further interviewers, and switching on automations. Two things stay yours alone:

  • Account & Billing does not appear in their account menu.
  • They cannot remove the account owner. That chip has no × in their view.
Add deliberately

Because access is all or nothing, adding somebody to interview also lets them change the event's setup. Add the people who need to interview, and use update recipients for anyone who only needs to stay informed.

Removing an interviewer

  1. Open Interview settings from any of the three places above.
  2. Select the × on that person's chip. It disappears straight away and the count drops by one.
  3. Select Update to save it.

There is no confirmation step, so check you have removed the right person before saving. As with adding, nothing changes until you select Update — closing the panel leaves them on the event.
