Decisions for Scott (each one is a one-line change)

  1. Headline font. Rezi uses PP Neue Montreal, a paid font. This page uses Inter Tight (Inter with tighter metrics) at 500, which keeps the Inter rule. If the exact face matters, Neue Montreal is a licence from Pangram Pangram.
  2. Dark band colour. Rezi's dark band is near-black (#151515). The standing rule says navy for dark bands, so the two dark sections are navy. Switching to near-black is one token.
  3. Hero visual. Rezi's hero is a product frame, so the hero is the browser frame cycling through four product screens; the live candidate photo (your 9 Oct rule for the hero) now sits in the closing band. Swapping the photo back into the hero is a section change, not a page change.
  4. Hero action bar. Rezi's bar is "Upload your resume"; ours is "Browse by event type" with the five types. All chips go to the calendar until Brandon maps the type filter.
  5. Logos. Done 22 Oct: full-colour company marks (Scott confirmed permission).
  6. "87% of employers return" from the 9 Oct page is not on the live site, so it is not on this page.
  7. "Learn more" on the three product bands goes to the FAQ page for now.
