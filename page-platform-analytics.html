Platform · Analytics & reporting

When the event ends, the report is already written.

Nothing is compiled afterwards. Every request, every accepted interview, every reschedule and every no-show is counted as your recruiters work, so the post-event report — interview stats, candidate dispositions, downloadable data — is ready the moment the event is over.

  1. 01 — During

    It counts as it happens

    A candidate requests, you accept, the interview runs or it does not, and your recruiter records what they thought. Each of those is already a number.

  2. 02 — The moment it ends

    The report is ready

    Interview stats, candidate dispositions and downloadable data. You do not request it, wait for it, or ask an account manager to pull it.

  3. 03 — After

    It keeps counting

    Follow-up interviews and messaging continue after the event, and the dashboard keeps dispositions, notes and analytics against each candidate.

The real screen

Four numbers, one chart, and a row for every event.

This is the Analytics view for interviews, unretouched — one account, one date range. Hover a pin, or a caption, to see which part of the screen it belongs to.

About that 66%. The attendance rate on this screen belongs to one account over one date range — 04/01/2026 to 05/20/2026 — and it is a sample, not a platform figure. Show rates published elsewhere on this site are platform-wide numbers based on 2025 data, and they will not match what any single dashboard says, including yours. That is rather the point of the report: it tells you what happened at your events, not what happens on average.

Proof 01

What the report contains, before you open anything.

Four counts sit across the top of the screen, and all four move with the date range. Three of them are what the platform observed. The fourth is what your recruiters decided.

  1. 01 Interviews scheduled Every request you accepted. Accepting is the scheduling step, so this is a count of decisions you made. 90 in this range
  2. 02 Attendance rate How much of what you booked actually took place, across the range you chose. Your rate, not an average. 66% in this range
  3. 03 Missed interviews The ones that did not happen — on the same row of cards as the good news, at the same size. 31 in this range
  4. 04 Marked yes The dispositions your recruiters recorded after the interview. The one number on this screen that is your judgement. 19 in this range

Proof 02

Requested, scheduled, completed — as one picture.

Requests are not applications and scheduled is not the same as completed. The chart draws Interviews scheduled against Interviews completed on the same axis, so the distance between the two lines is the only figure you have to interpret.

  • Granularity is a control, not a fixed view: Event day, Day, Week or Month.
  • The chart's own footnote: “Includes rescheduled interviews shown on their actual interview date.” A moved interview counts on the day it happened, not the day it was first booked.
  • Requests and scheduled are genuinely different columns, because a candidate requesting time and you accepting are two different events.
  • Where the interview happened does not change the count. JobFairX video, phone, your own link or your own address all land in the same series.

Proof 03

One row per event. Eight columns, no blending.

A single blended attendance rate hides which event earned it. The Events table at the foot of the same screen breaks the identical period out event by event, so two events in two cities are never averaged into each other.

  • EVENT · DATE · REQUESTS · SCHEDULED · RESCHEDULED · COMPLETED · MISSED · MARKED YES
  • The dropdown filters the table to a single event, or leaves it on All events.
  • Rescheduled gets a column of its own, so a moved interview is not quietly counted as a miss.
  • Interview volume by package: 20+ scheduled interviews on Starter, 60+ on Growth, 100+ on Pro. The SCHEDULED column is where you find out.

Proof 04

Downloadable data, and a dashboard that stays open.

The post-event report is not a summary you read once. It arrives with interview stats, candidate dispositions and downloadable data — and the screen it came from does not close when the event does.

  • Post-event report with interview stats, candidate dispositions, and downloadable data — on the All Packages Include list, so Starter, Growth and Pro alike.
  • Résumés and contact info for every matched candidate, not only the ones who turned up.
  • The employer dashboard keeps dispositions, notes and analytics against each candidate.
  • Follow-up interviews and messaging continue after the event, which is why the numbers on this screen keep moving after the day itself.
