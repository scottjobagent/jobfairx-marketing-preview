Platform · Event day

Event day is a queue you work down.

The lobby shows who is waiting and for how long, who is in a room, and who you are due to see next — whatever interview location you set. Start interview sits right on the row.

Register for an Event Browse upcoming events →

  1. 01 — Waiting to interview

    They turn up

    A candidate whose interview you accepted arrives at their slot and lands in the lobby. Their wait time starts counting on the row.

  2. 02 — Interviewing

    You start the room

    The row carries the name, the desired job, the desired location and the interview time. Status reads Ready, and Start interview opens the room.

  3. 03 — Interviewed

    The count moves

    When the call ends the candidate lands under Interviewed. That tab count is your running total for the day, visible without leaving the lobby.

The real screen

The lobby, live, two hours and forty-two minutes from the end.

This is the event-day lobby, unretouched, mid-event. Hover a pin — or a caption — to see which part of the screen it belongs to.

The lobby is one screen per event. Candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, so “desired location” on these rows is a local answer — Dallas, TX or Remote, not a national one.

Four tabs, four counts

Every candidate is on exactly one of them.

The counts across the top are the state of your event, and they move on their own as the day runs. You work the first tab; the other three are the score.

  1. 6 on this screen Waiting to interview Here now, wait time running. This tab splits into Interview rooms — ready to start — and Waiting rooms. ReadyEntering interview roomAwaiting available interviewer
  2. 3 on this screen Interviewing In session right now — three interviews running at the same time, across your recruiter seats.
  3. 5 on this screen Interviewed Finished. The count is your running total for the day, and it is the number the post-event report is built from.
  4. 26 on this screen Not yet interviewed Everyone else on your list for this event who has not been through a room yet.

The waiting tab

Rooms run in parallel. Your seats decide how many.

The waiting tab is two tables, not one. Interview rooms holds the candidates a free interviewer can take right now; Waiting rooms holds the rest.

Two kinds of waiting

The status on each waiting row tells you which kind it is — and two of these three are waiting on a person, not on a process. The wait time is on the same line.

Entering interview roomMoving through. Nothing for you to do.
Awaiting available interviewerEvery seat is busy — the status that costs you a candidate, and the wait time beside it is how much it has cost so far.
Seats by tier2 recruiter seats on Starter, up to 5 on Growth, unlimited recruiters on Pro.
Your status: AvailableIn the lobby header — each recruiter says when they are free rather than being assumed to be.

Inside the room

Notes, résumé and chat sit beside the call.

The candidate's name and the job they want are on screen, a timer runs from the moment the room opens, and the tools you need during the conversation share the bar with mute and End. No second tab, no second app.

Interview locations

Phone or in person? Same queue.

The event is a market moment — Dallas Healthcare Hiring Event, Apr 22, 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM CT — and every employer joins that same one. What you set is where your own interviews happen, and the lobby header carries it.

The queue does not change shape

On this screen the header reads Video · JobFairX video call. Another employer at this same Dallas event reads something else — and the four tabs, the two tables, the wait times and the statuses are the same screen either way.

When the countdown ends

At 3:00 the screen changes job.

When the countdown runs out the header reads Ended, the tabs become Summary, Interviewed and Not interviewed — the page stops being a queue and starts being a handover.
