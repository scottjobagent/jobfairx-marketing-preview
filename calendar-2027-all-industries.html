What this is

A new 2027 format, delivered as new files. Each market runs one All-Industries hiring event per usable week. Every event contains five rooms — Veterans, Entry-Level, Technology, Healthcare, Diversity — so a job seeker registers once and an employer in any vertical has an event every week in every city. The previous calendar (one event type per week, five dated events per city per month) is unchanged and remains at calendar-2027.html; it is a separate deliverable, not superseded by this page until the owner says so.

Import the rows of the two CSVs below as scheduled events and surface them on the hiring-event calendar page using the existing stickers for the five rooms. Nothing here changes the event page, registration flow or pricing.

04

Scheduling rules

  • Every usable week, one event per market. A usable week has at least two open Tue/Wed/Thu days. A week with only one open day (the week after New Year, the Eid week in March, the last week of Passover, the week of Nov 30) is skipped: it would put every market on a single day, which the owner has ruled out. Months therefore carry 3, 4 or 5 events per market.
  • Spread across the week. Within each week a market's event lands on Tue, Wed or Thu by a rotation that moves month to month, so no market is always on the same weekday and each day carries about a third of the markets.
  • Five rooms in every event, always the same five, in the same order.
  • Tue/Wed/Thu only; 11:00–15:00 local; early registration closes 30 days out.
  • Week structure is inherited verbatim from the verified per-type calendar — the same 45 US and 46 Canadian weeks, the same open days. Only what happens inside a week changed.
05

Holiday policy

Unchanged from the per-type calendar. All 44 US federal and religious observances are hard blocks; the US-only windows are Jul 5–9, Nov 22–28 and Dec 18–31. State closures move only that state's markets within the week: Feb 9 Mardi Gras (Louisiana; Mobile AL), Mar 31 Cesar Chavez Day (California), Nov 2 Election Day (14 states). Apr 21 carries an informational Erev Pesach note; the 11–3 window ends before sundown.

Canada swaps the US federal layer for its own: Jul 1–2, Sep 30, Nov 11 and Oct 11–15 closed nationally, Dec 18–31 closed, and Jun 24 (Fête nationale) handled for Quebec. Every other Canadian statutory holiday in 2027 falls on a Monday.
