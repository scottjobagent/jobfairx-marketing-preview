01

What to build

Import the rows of the two event files (download links at the top of this page and in §02) as scheduled hiring events, one per row, and show them on the hiring-event calendar page (/employer/hiring-event-calendar) with the existing event-type stickers. Each row is one event in one market on one date. Nothing here changes the event page, registration flow or pricing; it is schedule data only.

The Calendar, All Cities and Breakdown tabs on this page show how the schedule should read. They are a reference, not a component to ship.

Eventbrite: load every event except Diversity. Skip the rows whose event_type is Diversity. One listing of each of the other four types in every market is 214 × 4 = 856 listings, under Eventbrite's limit of 1,000 events listed at a time. When an event ends, list the next event of that type in the same market. The JobFairX website shows all five types.

04

Scheduling rules

The generator enforces these. A row that breaks one is a bug.

  1. Every market gets all five event types every month, once each. 189 × 5 × 12 = 11,340 in the US; 25 × 5 × 12 = 1,500 in Canada.
  2. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday only, 11:00 to 15:00 local. Never a Monday, Friday or weekend.
  3. One event type per week, the same type everywhere. A four-week month runs week 1 Veterans, week 2 Entry-Level, week 3 Healthcare, week 4 Technology and Diversity together. A three-week month has no week 4: Healthcare joins Veterans in week 1, week 2 stays Entry-Level only, and Technology and Diversity close the month. A five-week month runs one type a week in that order. types_this_week says how many types run in a week.
  4. March is the one exception. Its last week is Mar 30–31 only, and Mar 31 is Cesar Chavez Day, when California's events must move. Two types on two days would leave California nowhere to move to, so in March Technology and Diversity run in week 3 (Mar 24–25) and Healthcare in week 4 (Mar 30–31).
  5. Each week's markets are spread across its usable days: about 63 US events a day in a one-type week with three open days, about 126 in a two-type week. A two-type week with only two open days (Jan 12–14 has three; Apr 20–21, Mar 24–25 and Dec 1–2 have two) puts an event in every market on both days.
  6. A market's weekday rotates month to month (Austin: 21 Tuesdays, 20 Wednesdays, 19 Thursdays). Each market has a fixed place in the rotation (rotation in the market files), so adding or removing a market never moves another market's dates.
  7. A market never has two events on one day.
  8. Closed windows. US: Jul 5–9, Nov 22–28, Dec 18–31. Canada: Jul 1–2, Oct 11–15, Dec 18–31.
  9. No events on a blocked observance in jobfairx-2027-holidays.csv.
  10. A week with only one usable day is skipped. Weeks are cut at month edges, so a lone day at a month's edge is skipped too.

05

Holiday policy

All 44 US federal and religious observances in 2027 are hard blocks; only 11 of them fall on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday. “No events on any religious holiday” cannot be applied literally, because nearly every date touches some observance (every day of Ramadan, for example). An independent audit of 617 observances produced the tiers below.

Tier A A state closes: only its markets move, within the same week

DateClosureUS events movedMoved to
Tue Feb 9Mardi Gras: Louisiana markets, and Mobile, AL3Feb 11
Wed Mar 31Cesar Chavez Day: California markets11Mar 30
Tue Nov 2Election Day: markets in VA, NY, NJ, HI, IL, IN, KY, LA, MD, MI, MT, RI (DE and WV have none)31Nov 3 (17) or Nov 4 (14)

Apr 21 is the eve of Passover; the first Seder is at sundown. Events stay, because the 11:00–15:00 window ends well before it, and each carries an informational note.

Tier B (a large community affected, nothing closes; not acted on): Apr 14 Vaisakhi, Jun 15 Ashura, Sep 15 Mid-Autumn Festival and Chuseok, Sep 16 Mexican Independence Day, Oct 7 Durga Ashtami, Oct 28 Deepavali (South Indian date), Dec 8 Immaculate Conception. Tier C (observed, a normal working day) is ignored.

Canada drops the US federal days and closes Jul 1–2, Sep 30, Oct 11–15, Nov 11 and Dec 18–31 nationally. Jun 24 (Fête nationale) applies to Quebec only, and no Quebec market is on the list. Every other Canadian statutory holiday in 2027 falls on a Monday.

Estimated dates. Islamic dates (Eid al-Fitr Mar 9–10, Eid al-Adha May 16–17) depend on moon sighting, and Hindu dates (Holi Mar 22, Diwali Oct 29) need astronomical confirmation. Re-check them in early 2027; a one-day shift of Eid al-Fitr can change a March week.
06

Integration notes

  • Labels match the live stickers exactly: Veterans, Entry-Level, Healthcare, Technology, Diversity.
  • Eventbrite gets four of the five types. Leave every Diversity row off Eventbrite; load the rest (§01).
  • Dates are local. Store date as a date, not a UTC timestamp, or events can drift a day.
  • Time zones. Every event runs 11:00–15:00 in the market's own time, and the files have no time-zone column: resolve it per market from the ZIP or postal code. States that span two zones (TX, FL, TN, KY, IN, ND, SD, NE, KS, OR, ID) resolve per city. British Columbia stopped changing its clocks in 2026 and stays on UTC−7 all year, so its winter events are not on Pacific Standard Time. Saskatchewan has no daylight saving time.
  • Import key. Use event_id. Because it contains the date, a file regenerated after a schedule change is not a clean upsert: remove the affected markets' old 2027 rows first.
  • Early registration closes on early_reg_deadline, 30 days before the event.
  • Area names. Inland Empire, Long Island and Orange County are areas, not cities; they have past sales and stay. Nepean, ON is inside Ottawa, which is also listed; it is flagged (review_flag) and scheduled.
