Platform · Messaging & automations

The follow-up nobody has time for, already sent.

Every conversation with a candidate sits in one inbox, filtered by event and by job. Beside it are five automation presets — in the product's own words, messages that send as candidates move through your hiring process. You decide which ones are on, and what each one covers.

  1. 01 — Presets

    Turn one on

    Five presets sit on the Automations screen. Status on each row is a single On or Off, and it is set per automation — not for the account.

  2. 02 — Scope

    Point it somewhere

    Jobs included takes one job or all of them. Hiring event takes one event or all of them. The two columns sit on every row.

  3. 03 — Send

    It sends as they move

    Nothing to trigger by hand. The messages go as candidates move through the pipeline, and they land in the same inbox you type into.

The real screen

One inbox, scoped by event and by job.

This is the Messages screen, unretouched, cropped to the conversation column. Hover a pin — or a caption — to see which part of the screen it belongs to.

The two dropdowns above the list are the same two controls the Automations table uses — it filters by job, and every row names the jobs it includes and the hiring event it belongs to. Scope in one place, scope in the other.

Proof 01

Five presets are ready to turn on.

They are not a workflow you have to build. They are five rows in a table, each with a Status you flip, a set of jobs it covers and a hiring event it belongs to. Three are On and two are Off in the screen below — nothing here is all-or-nothing.

  1. 01 Message new candidates The opening message on a new conversation, sent without you writing it. On1 jobDallas Healthcare
  2. 02 Message scheduled candidates For candidates whose interview is booked. Scheduled is a pipeline state. On3 jobsChicago Technology
  3. 03 Missed interview follow-up For the interview nobody joined. No-show is a pipeline state, and this is not a dead end. OnAll jobsAll events
  4. 04 Email declined candidates For the candidates you passed on. Declined is a pipeline state too. Off2 jobsAtlanta Diversity
  5. 05 Post-interview follow-up After the interview is done. Interviewed is a pipeline state; this is the message that comes next. Off1 jobDallas Healthcare

The Status, job count and event on each card above are the values on the real screen — one account's five rows, not a fixed configuration. Every preset can be On or Off, scoped to one job or all of them, at one event or all of them.

Proof 02

The screen states its own job in one line.

“Messages that send as candidates move through your hiring process.” That is the product's description, not ours, and it is printed at the top of the Automations screen. Below it are five rows and one decision per row.

  • Status is On or Off, per automation. Not a global switch, and not a plan you have to be on.
  • Automation name · Jobs included · Hiring event · Date modified — four columns, so the last change is on the row you are reading.
  • Presets cover the moments a recruiter running an event never gets back to: new, scheduled, missed, declined, and after the interview.
  • One layer up, the same principle applies to the requests themselves — auto-accept or review each request is on the All Packages Include list, at every tier.

Proof 03

Point one at a single job. Point the next at everything.

Two columns carry the targeting, and they are independent. An automation can cover one job at one hiring event; the one below it can cover all jobs at all events. You are not choosing a single policy for the account.

  • Jobs included reads 1 job, 3 jobs or 2 jobs on the specific rows — or All jobs.
  • Hiring event names one event — Dallas Healthcare, Chicago Technology, Atlanta Diversity — or reads All events.
  • Your job count is the tier you registered on: 1 job on Starter, up to 3 on Growth, up to 6 on Pro.
  • Seats scale the same way — 2 recruiter seats on Starter, up to 5 on Growth, unlimited on Pro.

Proof 04

The event ends. The messaging does not.

Follow-up interviews and messaging after the event are on the “All Packages Include” list — Starter, Growth and Pro alike. The inbox is not scoped to a live event, and a thread leaves it when you archive it, not when the clock runs out.

  • Schedule follow-up interviews and message candidates after the event.
  • Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event, in every package.
  • Résumés and contact info for every matched candidate stay with you — not only the ones you interviewed.
  • A post-event report arrives with recruiter notes, interview records and résumés, as downloadable data.

The rest of the platform

What the messages are about.

Events dashboard → Every event you have registered for, with the interview location you set on each one. Candidate pipeline → Five states — Requested, Scheduled, Interviewed, No-show, Declined. The presets follow them. Interview scheduling → Accepted requests land on one schedule, whichever interview location they belong to. Event day → The lobby, the interview itself, and what the screen shows once a session has ended. Analytics & reporting → Dispositions, notes and analytics, plus the post-event report with downloadable data. The platform, end to end → All six capabilities on one page, in the order a hiring event actually runs.
