Solutions · Diversity

For employers building diverse teams, one market at a time.

Diversity is one of the five JobFairX event types. An event is a market moment — one city, one date, one four-hour window — and every employer joins the same one. Candidates registered for it are verified within approximately 20 miles of the event city and matched against the jobs you posted. They request time with you, and accepting a request is what schedules the interview. Where that interview happens is yours to set: JobFairX video, phone, in person at your address, or your own link.

End to end

How a diversity event runs.

The same four beats as every other type. What changes is which event you registered for — and, if you want it to, where your interviews happen.

  1. 01 — Register

    Pick the event

    Choose the diversity event in your city and a package, then post your jobs. Registering 31 or more days out takes a flat $100 off any tier.

  2. 02 — Set up

    Set your interview location

    JobFairX video, phone, in person at your address, or your own link. It is a column on your events dashboard and it is yours alone — the employer in the next row of the same event can choose differently.

  3. 03 — Match

    Requests start arriving

    Matching activates the moment your jobs are posted and the first interview requests land within a few hours. Auto-accept them, or review each one — both are in every package.

  4. 04 — Interview

    Accept, and it is booked

    The request already carries a time, so accepting is the scheduling step. Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event are included at every tier.

  • Job titles are the column candidates match against, which is why the posted title needs to be the title you are actually hiring for.
  • How many you can post is a package line: 1 job on Starter, up to 3 on Growth, up to 6 on Pro.
  • Auto-accept or review each request — both are in every package, at every tier. Reviewing means you read the résumé before anything is booked.
  • Résumés and contact information for every matched candidate are included, along with a post-event report with downloadable data.

What you see

What a candidate row tells you.

The columns are Name, Desired job, Desired location, Time and Actions. A row carries the job they want, where they want to work, the slot they asked for, and your three buttons. That is the whole record you act on.

  • Accept is the scheduling step. The request already carries a time, so one click books it and the row moves to Scheduled.
  • Desired location is a local answer. Candidates are verified within approximately 20 miles of the event city.
  • View resume and Message sit on every row, in every state — including the ones you declined and the ones who did not show.
  • Which candidates you interview is your decision, taken row by row on this screen.
