Four ways to interview

Same event. Your choice of room.

Every employer joins the same hiring event. Where your own interviews happen is a setting you control, event by event — so two employers at one event can interview completely differently.

The dashboard reads “JobFairX video call”. The interview runs inside the platform — nothing to install, for you or the candidate.

The dashboard reads “You call each candidate”, at the time you accepted. Candidates are told to expect your call.

Your street address — “1201 Peachtree St NE, Suite 300 · Atlanta, GA” — shown on the dashboard and on every candidate confirmation and reminder.

The dashboard names the tool — “Microsoft Teams”. Paste your own link and JobFairX schedules around it.

Matched candidates request the interview

Matching activates the moment your jobs are posted. The first requests arrive within a few hours, each carrying the job, the location and the slot they asked for.

One click accepts — and schedules it

The request already carries a time, so accepting is the booking. The row moves to Scheduled at that exact slot and the candidate is told. There is no second step.

Your interview location, per event

JobFairX video, phone, in person at your street address, or your own Teams or Zoom link. Candidates see exactly what to expect on every confirmation and reminder.

How the schedule fills

Requests come to you. Accepting books them.

You are not searching a database or chasing replies. Matched candidates ask for time against the jobs you posted, and every request you accept lands on your schedule at the slot they chose.
