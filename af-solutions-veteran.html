Solutions — Veteran hiring events

Hire Mission-Ready Veterans

Interview reliable, well-trained veterans matched to your open roles. They request the interview at the veteran hiring event in your market; accepting a request is what schedules it. Candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, so everyone on your list is already local.

Register for an Event Browse veteran events →
01 — Register

Take a place at the event

Pick the veteran hiring event in your city and post your open roles. Matching activates immediately and the first interview requests arrive within a few hours.

02 — Requested

Veterans request you

A matched candidate asks for time with you. The request carries the job they want, where they want to work, and the slot they asked for — so there is nothing to sift and nobody to chase.

03 — Scheduled

You accept, it is booked

One click on the row schedules the interview at that time. Where it happens is your call, set once per event: JobFairX video, phone, in person at your address, or your own link.

Interview locations

One event. You decide where your interviews happen.

A veteran hiring event is a market moment — one city, one date, one window — and every employer joins the same one. What you set independently is your interview location. Two employers at the same event can run it completely differently: one on video, one with candidates walking into their office.

One schedule

The day lands on one schedule, whichever room each interview is in.

Every request you accept appears on the Interviews tab in time order, each line carrying its own interview-location tag. Video, phone and in-person interviews sit in the same list, because they belong to the same event.

One schedule, three tabs

Upcoming, Pending and Past, with an all-events filter above them — every accepted request lands here in time order.

  • Reschedule proposes a new time; Cancel is one click, on the same line.
  • Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event are included in every package.
  • Volume by tier: 20+ scheduled interviews on Starter, 60+ on Growth, 100+ on Pro.

The one number that exists

89% of scheduled interviews at veteran events are kept.

That is the one veteran-specific figure we hold, and it is the one that decides whether a hiring day is worth booking. Show rate is measured per audience: healthcare runs at 91%, veteran at 89%, entry-level at 86% — same platform, different rooms.

  • 87% of employers come back for another event, across all five audiences.
  • Interview counts and hires are per event and vary by market — each event page carries its own live figures, updated daily.
  • Rather than reuse another audience's averages here, the veteran totals are left blank until they are measured.
