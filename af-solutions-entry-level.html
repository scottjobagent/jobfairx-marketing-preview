End to end

How an entry-level event runs.

One market moment — a city, a date, a four-hour window. Every employer joins the same event. What each of you sets independently is where your own interviews happen.

  1. 01 — Register

    Pick the event

    Choose the entry-level event in your city and a package, then post your open roles. Registering 31 or more days out takes a flat $100 off any tier.

  2. 02 — Set up

    Set your interview location

    JobFairX video, phone, in person at your address, or your own link. It is a column on your events dashboard, and it is yours alone — the employer in the next row of the same event can choose differently.

  3. 03 — Match

    Requests start arriving

    Matching activates the moment your jobs are posted and the first interview requests land within a few hours. Auto-accept them, or review each one — both are in every package.

  4. 04 — Interview

    Accept, and it is booked

    The request already carries a time, so accepting is the scheduling step. Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event are included at every tier.

Interview location is per employer, per event. The column reads a product name, a phone instruction or a street address depending on what that employer chose. It is not an attribute of the event.
Seats and jobs are the package on screen. “1 of 3 jobs, 1 of 5 seats” is Growth. Starter is 1 job and 2 recruiter seats; Pro is up to 6 jobs and unlimited recruiters — which is the tier volume hiring lands on.
Job titles are what candidates match against. Whatever you post is what the matching works from, so the posted title needs to be the title you would actually hire.
Pending versus upcoming. Pending are requests waiting on you. Upcoming are the ones you already accepted, which means they are already scheduled.
