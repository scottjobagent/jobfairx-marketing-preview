How it works

Four things happen, and you control all of them

Nothing about this event is a lucky dip. You decide how your team interviews, which candidates get time, and what every one of them was worth afterwards.

Step one

Post your roles and choose how you interview

Register, post the jobs you need to fill, and set your interview location. This is your setting, not the event’s — another employer at the same event can interview a different way. Matching starts as soon as your jobs are live.

  • In person at your Austin address, with parking and check-in notes that reach candidates with their confirmation
  • On JobFairX video, in the browser, with nothing to install and no link to send
  • By phone, calling each candidate at the time you accepted

Whichever you choose, it never changes the price.

Step two

Candidates ask you for a time, and you say yes

Post your roles and AI matches them to Austin-area candidates who registered for this date. Matched candidates request a slot that suits them. You accept it, and accepting is what books the interview. There is no chasing, and no resume pile to work through first.

  • Take each request individually, or turn on auto-accept and let the day fill itself
  • Every request arrives with a resume and contact details
  • Candidates get reminders by email and SMS, which is why 91% of them turn up

Step three · September 30, 2026

You run the day from your interview rooms

Your lobby opens at 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM CDT. Candidates check themselves in, so you can see who is ready now and who is still waiting. You interview from your rooms, and your team can run several at once, up to the seats in your package. Where those interviews happen is your call, set once when you register: in person at your Austin address, on JobFairX video with nothing to install, or by phone.

Step four

Every interview comes back with a decision on it

JobFairX does not guess who you liked. Each completed interview carries the rating your own interviewer gave it, yes, maybe or no, alongside who ran it, the notes they took and where it happened. Message a candidate or book a second round from the same row.
