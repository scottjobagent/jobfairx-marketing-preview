Interview locations

Same event. Your choice of room.

An event is a market moment — the Dallas Healthcare Hiring Event, on its date, at its time. Every employer registered joins that same event. What you set independently is your interview location, and it is a column on your dashboard.

Two employers at the same event can interview completely differently. In-person is not an event type and never has been — it is one of four places your own interviews can happen, set per event, changeable while you are still in setup. And because candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city, an in-person interview is a local ask, not a trip. How interview locations work →
