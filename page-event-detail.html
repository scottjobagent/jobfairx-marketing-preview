FAQ

Questions about this event.

What is early registration pricing?

Registering 30 or more days before the event saves $100 on every package. For the September 22 event, early registration ends August 22 — after that all three packages return to their standard price.

Are the candidates local to Pearland?

Yes. Candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city. That holds however you choose to interview — it's what makes in-person interviews practical here without shrinking your pool.

When does candidate matching start?

As soon as you register and post your roles. Candidate matching activates immediately and you will start receiving interview requests within a few hours.

How do candidates reach me?

They come to you. Matched candidates request an interview from the times you've made available — there is no pile for you to work through. You accept, decline, or propose a new time; accepting schedules the interview and notifies them.

Where do the interviews actually happen?

Wherever you decide. Each employer sets its own interview location for this event: the JobFairX video room, phone, in person at your address, or your own Zoom, Teams or Meet link. Two employers at this same event can interview completely differently.

How many interviews should I expect?

It depends on your package: Starter is built around 20+ scheduled interviews, Growth 60+, and Pro 100+. Employers at this event have averaged 35 interviews and 7 hires.

Can I interview before or after September 22?

Yes. Every package includes unlimited interviews before, during and after the event, plus follow-up interviews and messaging once it ends. You also get a post-event report with downloadable data.
