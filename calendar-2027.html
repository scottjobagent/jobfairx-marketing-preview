01

What to build

Import the 20,700 rows of jobfairx-2027-calendar.csv as scheduled hiring events, one per row, and surface them on the existing hiring-event calendar page (/employer/hiring-event-calendar) using the existing event-type stickers. Each row is one virtual event in one market on one date. Nothing here changes the event page, registration flow, or pricing — it is schedule data only.

The Calendar, All Cities and Breakdown tabs on this page are the reference for how the schedule should read. They are a design reference, not a component to ship.

04

Scheduling rules

These are the rules the generator enforces, in the order they apply. A row that violates any of them is a bug.

  1. Every market gets all five event types every month, exactly once each. 345 × 5 × 12 = 20,700. No exceptions.
  2. Events run only on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Never Monday, Friday, or a weekend.
  3. One event type per week. A calendar week (Mon–Sun) carries one type nationally, except that some weeks carry two — five types will not divide evenly into four weeks. types_this_week says which.
  4. Types run in a fixed order through every month: Veterans → Entry-Level → Technology → Healthcare → Diversity. Week 1 is always Veterans.
  5. Within a week, the 345 markets are spread across that week's usable days so no day carries the whole country. A normal week is ~115 events/day; a two-type week ~230.
  6. A market's weekday rotates month to month. No market is permanently on a Thursday. (Austin: 21 Tue, 20 Wed, 19 Thu across the year.)
  7. A market never has two events on one day.
  8. Closed windows, no events: Jul 5–9 (Independence week), Nov 22–28 (Thanksgiving week), Dec 18–31.
  9. No events on any of the 44 federal or religious observances in jobfairx-2027-holidays.csv.
  10. A week with only one usable day is skipped rather than stacking 345 events on it. This is why the week count varies by month.

05

Holiday policy

An independent audit rebuilt the 2027 calendar for every major tradition from scratch (617 observances across federal, Western Christian, Orthodox, Jewish, Islamic, Hindu/Sikh/Buddhist/Jain/Baha'i, plus diaspora civic days) and checked every event date against it. Every anchor date — Easter, Passover, Rosh Hashanah, both Eids, Diwali, Thanksgiving — was independently recomputed and confirmed correct.

The instruction “no events on any religious holiday” is not achievable literally. the great majority of event dates touch some observance somewhere (every day of Ramadan, for instance). The policy below is what was actually applied. It is a business decision and the tiers can be moved.

Tier A A US jurisdiction closes — affected markets move, same week

These are state closures, so only markets in that state move. The national date stands for everyone else. 93 events carry a holiday_note saying where they came from.

Tier B Large community affected, no closure — 86 findings, not acted on

Worth knowing for city-targeted marketing, not for the national schedule. The ones with the widest reach: Apr 14 Vaisakhi + Tamil/Lao/Khmer/Thai new years · Jun 15 Ashura · Sep 15–16 Mid-Autumn/Chuseok + Mexican & Central American Independence · Oct 7 Durga Ashtami · Oct 28 Deepavali (South Indian date) · Dec 8 Immaculate Conception. Full list with severity and reasoning in jobfairx-2027-holiday-audit.csv.

Tier C Observed but a normal US working day — 187 findings, ignored

One correction from final verification: Purim was initially computed as Feb 21. 5787 is a Hebrew leap year, so Purim is 14 Adar II = Tue Mar 23; Feb 21 is Purim Katan. The block list and the schedule were corrected and re-verified. Independent adversarial check afterward: 9 rules, all hold.
Two dates in the block list are estimates, not calculations. Islamic dates (Eid al-Fitr Mar 9–10, Eid al-Adha May 16–17) depend on moon sighting and can shift a day; Hindu dates (Holi Mar 22, Diwali Oct 29) need astronomical confirmation. The audit confirmed all four as currently expected. Re-check in early 2027; if Eid al-Fitr moves, March regenerates.
06

Integration notes

  • Event type labels must match the live stickers exactly: Veterans (plural), Entry-Level (hyphenated), Technology, Healthcare, Diversity.
  • Timezone. date is the local calendar date in the market. Store as a date, not a UTC timestamp, or events near midnight will drift a day.
  • Every event runs 11:00–15:00 in the market's local time. The same clock time applies in every zone, so a Tuesday's events open in a rolling wave from Eastern to Hawaii. States spanning two zones (TX, FL, TN, KY, IN, ND, SD, NE, KS, OR, ID) resolve per city from zip_code.
  • Idempotent import. Key on event_id. Re-importing a regenerated file should upsert, not duplicate.
  • Early registration opens at file load and closes on early_reg_deadline (30 days out). Standard registration runs to the event date.
  • Three markets are flagged in jobfairx-2027-markets.csv (review_flag): Redwood CA (no such city; Redwood City is separate), Clinton MI (township vs village), Abbeville AL (pop. 2,370). They are scheduled; the flag is for the business to resolve.
  • The four “United States” region pages (Central, Eastern, Western, National) are not in the file. They were excluded as landing pages. If they should host events, that is 240 more rows.
