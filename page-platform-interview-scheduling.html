Proof 01

Three tabs. Answered, unanswered, done.

The whole scheduling model is two numbers next to each other. Pending is what you owe candidates. Upcoming is what you have committed to. There is no third system holding a copy of either.

Proof 02

Accept. Decline. Or propose a new time.

The request already carries a time, so accepting is not the first stage of an approval flow — it is the booking. The row leaves Pending and appears under Upcoming at that exact slot, and the candidate is told.

  • Accept schedules it. There is no confirmation step, no invite to send and no calendar to sync.
  • Reschedule proposes a different time; until the candidate confirms, the interview reads “Proposed · awaiting candidate”.
  • Decline closes the request out without a message you have to write.
  • Or skip the queue entirely: auto-accept or review each request is on the All Packages Include list, at every tier.

Proof 03

One schedule holds every interview location at once.

An event is a market moment — a city, an audience, a four-hour window — and every employer joins the same one. Where your interviews happen is yours to set, and the schedule does not care which you picked.

  • JobFairX video — the interview runs inside the platform.
  • Phone — you call each candidate.
  • In person — candidates come to the address you set, which matters when they are already verified within roughly 20 miles of the event city.
  • Your own link — bring the tool your team already runs on.

Proof 04

The messages around the interview send themselves.

Automations are messages that send as candidates move through your hiring process. Two of the five sit either side of the scheduling moment, so a booked interview does not need a recruiter to remember it.

  • Message scheduled candidates — scoped to exactly the moment a request becomes a booked interview.
  • Missed interview follow-up — for the ones who did not turn up, so a no-show is not a dead end.
  • Each automation is switched On or Off on its own row, scoped to specific jobs and to one hiring event or to all of them.
  • Alongside them: Message new candidates, Email declined candidates and Post-interview follow-up.

Proof 05

They pick the slot. You only answer it.

Every row on the schedule already has a time on it, because the candidate chose one when they requested the interview. That is the reason there is no back-and-forth: you are answering a time, not proposing one.

  • Slots run in half-hour blocks — 10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 12:30 — inside the event's own window.
  • Matching activates the moment your jobs are posted, and the first requests arrive within a few hours.
  • Volume by tier: 20+ scheduled interviews on Starter, 60+ on Growth, 100+ on Pro.
  • Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event are included in every package.
