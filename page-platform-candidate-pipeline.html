The real screen

One list. Every candidate, and what you owe them.

Proof 01

Five states. Every candidate is in exactly one.

The tab you are on decides which actions a row offers. Nothing is hidden behind a detail page, and no state is a dead end — a declined candidate still has an Accept button if you change your mind.

Proof 02

One click accepts. The same click schedules it.

The request already carries a time. Accept is not a first stage in an approval flow — it is the booking. The row leaves Requested and appears under Scheduled at that exact slot, and the candidate is told.

  • Reschedule proposes a different time; until they confirm, the row reads “Proposed · awaiting candidate”.
  • Decline closes it out without a message you have to write.
  • Where the interview happens is set once, per event — JobFairX video, phone, in person at your address, or your own link — so it is never part of this decision.
  • Unlimited interviews before, during and after the event are included in every package.

Proof 03

Auto-accept, or review each request.

Both are in every package, at every tier. High-volume teams turn matching loose and let the calendar fill; teams hiring one specialist read every résumé first. You are not choosing a plan to get either one.

  • Review each request — you get a notification for every request, and a summary email every morning at 9:00 AM.
  • Auto-accept — enable automatic interview scheduling and requests are accepted for you.
  • Matching activates the moment your jobs are posted, and the first requests arrive within a few hours.
  • Volume by tier: 20+ scheduled interviews on Starter, 60+ on Growth, 100+ on Pro.
