The fifteen landing pages behind the Solutions and Features menus

Built 23 Oct 2026. Feature pages take the product-page shape, opened without a picture since 23 October: a left-aligned hero with the pill, headline, line and buttons, the three benefit cards straight under it, the product capture in an "In the product" section below the cards, then three numbered steps, two FAQ questions, related features and a centered blue band. Hiring-event-type pages take the Enterprise shape with the hero reworked on 23 October: a white first screen with the live event page's own photo running to the right edge, the type's colour from the live calendar on the pill, its dot and the three averages (teal healthcare, blue technology, sky blue entry-level, orange diversity, red veterans), Reserve My Spot as the one brand-blue primary button, and the averages in a white card that overlaps the photo (then then the type's upcoming events, past employers, three employer quotes, how it works, the three packages, the six questions, and a band with the next event). Every sentence is live copy from jobfairx.com or the approved October product-update copy; the sources are listed per page. The band at the foot of each type page shows the event its live type page is selling (Scottsdale, Boise, Dallas, Birmingham, Dearborn) with that event's live candidate count, while the list above it is the live calendar's rows for the type. Titles and meta descriptions are drafts in the live pattern for Michael to confirm.

  • His tracker plans three of the five type pages as human-written wave-2 pages: /employer/healthcare-hiring (S-011), /employer/veteran-hiring (S-012), /employer/diversity-hiring (S-013), each with its main search term above. The designs use those addresses as file names. Technology and Entry-Level have no row; those two addresses follow the same pattern and are a question for him.
  • No feature pages exist in his plan. The only product page he plans is /employer/virtual-hiring-events (S-008, "virtual career fair software"). Addresses for the ten feature pages are a question for him; the designs use flat file names.
  • His rules that do apply are followed: "hiring events" wording, no trailing slash on /employer links, no query-string links, event links by slug, Reserve buttons that are not crawlable cart links (data-live="reserve"), live JSON-LD kept by Brandon.
  • Numbers on the type pages are the live type page's averages and candidate counts (data-dynamic where they change); the upcoming rows are the live calendar's rows for that type.
