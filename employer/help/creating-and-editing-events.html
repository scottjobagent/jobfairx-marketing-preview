Creating and editing events

Registering for an event, then everything on its edit page: the jobs you are interviewing for, how you want to interview, how many interviews you can run at once, and the settings that go with them.

The pencil on an event row opens Edit your Jobs & Settings, the single page that holds everything about how that event will run.

Back to dashboard sits above the page title, and the page is a stack of cards, each showing a one-line summary of its current state. Automations, Interview details, Screening, and Interviewers & updates carry a pencil that expands the card in place. Event details is fixed, and Interviewing For is already open.

Registering for an event

Register for an event replaces your events dashboard table with a list of upcoming JobFairX events. Three controls narrow the list: Search by city, an event-type dropdown that starts on All Types, and a country dropdown. Select Register on the row you want. Back to Dashboard returns you to your own events.

Posting and editing jobs

Interviewing For holds the jobs attached to this event. Its summary line shows your package and how many job slots you have used, for example Growth package · 2 of 3 jobs added. Each job appears as a card with its title, pay range, job type and the opening of its description, plus an Edit link.

Edit on a job card opens that posting on its own page, with fields for Job title, Location, Job type, Pay, Benefits, Standard Shift, Weekly Schedule and Job description, and a Save button.

Interview details: format, capacity and auto-accept

Interview details is the most important card on the page. Its summary line reads like “JobFairX Video interviews | 21 interviews across 6 time slots”, and expanding it reveals three settings.

1. How do you want to interview candidates?

Choose Video, Phone or In-Person. This is your event setting, and the page says so: “This applies to every interview scheduled for this event”. Every candidate who requests an interview comes in on the format you choose. What you choose changes what the platform asks for next:

  • Video offers two options. JobFairX video call is marked Recommended: no software to download and no links to manage, and JobFairX sends a secure video link to you and the candidate once the interview is confirmed. Third-party video conferencing lets you supply your own meeting link instead.
  • Phone needs nothing extra. When a candidate confirms, JobFairX sends you their phone number and you call them at the scheduled time.
  • In-Person asks for the Interview address, with a suite or unit if you have one, and a How to attend box for parking, which entrance to use and who to ask for. Both are included in every candidate's interview confirmation.
You can't change it after you publish

You choose the format while you set up the event. Once you publish your jobs for the event, it is locked and can't be changed, so choose it before you publish. See Choosing your interview location.

One interview can differ

The format above is the event setting. Rescheduling a single interview is the one place it can differ: when you propose a new time you can also change that interview's format, and the change applies to that interview only. Your event setting stays as it is, and every new request still arrives on it. See Interviews.

2. How many interviews can your team run in each time slot?

The event is divided into time slots of 15 or 30 minutes, depending on the slot length you chose. Each slot has its own minus and plus control, and Set every time slot to at the top applies one number across all of them at once, with an Undo if you change your mind. A slot already carrying interviews shows a count such as 2 booked. Setting a slot to zero marks it Closed, and candidates cannot book that time.

Underneath, the platform totals it up, for example 21 interviews across 6 open slots and “2 slots closed. Candidates cannot book those times.” It also warns you when a slot needs more people than you may have, in the form “Your busiest slot takes 4 interviews at once. Plan on 4 interviewers signed in at that time, or lower the number on that slot.”

Capacity is a promise

Every interview a slot allows is a slot a candidate can book. Set the number to what your team can genuinely staff at that moment, not to the maximum you would like to fill.

3. Auto accept interviews

Automatically accept interview requests? decides whether requests need your reply. With it on, candidates are confirmed instantly when they select a time slot, and auto-accept pauses on its own once all interview slots are full. With it off, every request lands under Interviews → Pending for you to accept, decline or reschedule.

Screening, resumes and interviewers

Three more cards finish the page.

  • Screening lets you add screening questions per job, so candidates answer your requirements up front and are filtered on their answers. The summary line counts how many of the event's jobs have screening, and expanding the card gives each job an Add screening button plus Add screening to multiple jobs.
  • Resume requirement is a single toggle: Require candidate to include resume?
  • Interviewers & updates summarises how many interviewers and daily update recipients the event has, and expands into the same email fields as the Interview settings dialog below.

Interview settings from the dashboard

You do not have to open the edit page to change who is interviewing. Selecting the interviewer count in the Interview setup column on the dashboard opens Interview settings over the top of the table, where you add interviewers by email and choose who receives updates. Update saves and closes; Cancel discards.

Adding interviewers covers every place you can add someone, how many interviewers your package includes, and what each person receives. Auto-accept is not in this dialog: it is on the edit page, under Interview details.
