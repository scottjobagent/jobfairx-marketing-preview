Testing video and audio

Check your microphone, speaker and camera before candidates arrive. The test lives inside the event lobby, and it takes about a minute.

Where the test lives

The test is attached to a specific event, so you reach it from that event's lobby rather than from a settings page.

  1. Open the event from your dashboard to reach its Lobby.
  2. Select the … button at the top right, beside the event title.
  3. Choose Test video and audio. It opens in a new window.

The same menu also holds Add interviewers and Update display name. There is a second route to the same place: the Test your audio and video before your event link that sits across the top of the lobby.

Allowing the browser first

The first thing the test window asks is To join the interview, allow microphone access. Select Give access, then accept your browser's own prompt. Nothing can be tested until the browser has permission, so this step is not optional — and it is worth doing well before the event rather than in the minute before your first interview.

What the test checks

Once permission is granted, the Test video and audio connection window opens on its Settings tab, with your own camera preview on the left and three panels on the right.

  • MicrophonePick your input and watch the Microphone Input meter move as you speak. There is a Suppress background noise option here.
  • SpeakerPick your output and use Test speaker to confirm you can hear it.
  • CameraPick your camera and check the preview. Mirror my video flips the preview for you only — candidates always see you the right way round.

A second tab, Backgrounds, lets you blur or replace what is behind you. That is a presentation choice rather than a connection check, but the same window is the place to set it before the day.

When a restricted network blocks it

Corporate networks are the most common cause of a failure that has nothing to do with your hardware. The test window carries a Network Access Reminder for exactly this, with two links:

  • Network testChecks whether your connection supports the video technology JobFairX uses. Run the network test →
  • Instructions for ITWhat your IT team needs to allow through the firewall, including the published domain and port list. Send this to IT →

If the network test fails, that is the result to forward — it tells your IT team what to change, and the test can be downloaded and attached.

Fixing a specific problem

Our public troubleshooting guide covers the individual failures in detail: camera and microphone permissions browser by browser, which browsers and devices are supported, and the allowlist your network team needs.

Troubleshooting the interview platform →

Two things worth knowing before you get there. JobFairX interviews need a modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari on a computer, Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome or Firefox on Android. Internet Explorer and Chrome on iOS will not work. And on a phone or tablet, switching to another app during an interview can release the microphone; if the candidate stops hearing you, restart the device and rejoin.

Still need help?

If the network test passes and you still cannot connect, contact JobFairX support with your event name and date, and attach the downloaded test result.
