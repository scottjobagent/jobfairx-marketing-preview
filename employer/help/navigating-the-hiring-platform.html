What you can do in the platform

The JobFairX employer platform is where you manage everything around a hiring event. From one account you can:

  • Register for upcoming hiring events, and keep track of the ones you have already booked.
  • Add the jobs you are interviewing for, and edit those postings at any time before the event.
  • Choose how you want to interview (video, phone or in person), whether interview time slots are 15 or 30 minutes, and how many interviews your team can run in each slot.
  • Add colleagues as interviewers so they can interview alongside you.
  • Accept, decline, reschedule and cancel interview requests from candidates.
  • Message candidates directly, and set up automated messages that send on their own.
  • Run the event day itself from the lobby: see who is waiting, start interviews, record feedback and notes.
  • Review how each event performed, and export the list of everyone who signed up.
Before you start

Every screenshot in this article is taken from the employer platform with a demo account called Northwind Co. Your event names, job titles and candidate names will differ, but the layout, labels and buttons are the same. The interface in these screenshots is dense, so select any one of them to open it full size in a new tab.

Start here: the events dashboard

Events is the first thing you see when you sign in, and it is the home base for everything else.

Your company name sits at the top of the page, with Register for an event beside it. Below that, two tabs split your events into Upcoming events and Past events, each showing a count. One row per event.

Reading a row

The six columns of the upcoming events table.
ColumnWhat it shows
Actions A pencil button that opens the event for editing, and a … menu with Edit, Share and Go to lobby. The setup status sits underneath.
Event details Event name, date, and start and end time with the time zone.
Interview location A chip reading Video, Phone or In person, with the specifics underneath: the venue address for an in-person event, JobFairX video call or the name of your own meeting platform for video, or You call each candidate for phone. A Go to lobby link sits below it.
Interview setup How many interviewers are on the event. Selecting the count opens Interview settings without leaving the dashboard, which is where you add interviewers. The line underneath compares that against the number your package includes, for example 2 of 5 interviewers.
Job titles The jobs you are interviewing for at that event, and a count such as 3 of 3 jobs. Selecting a title opens its public listing preview in a new tab.
Candidates How many people are waiting on your reply, how many interviews are confirmed, and a Manage candidates link into the lobby.

Filtering and the audio check

Search Hiring Events on the right of the tab row narrows the table to a single event. Between the tabs and that filter sits Test your audio and video before your event, which opens the audio and video check in a new tab. It is worth running once per machine before your first event, because your IT team may need to allow camera and microphone access first.

After the event: past events and the event report

The Past events tab lists events that have finished. A past-event row has no pencil, because there is nothing left to set up. Its … menu offers View Event Report, Go to lobby and Export Signups, and the Candidates column shows how many people you interviewed with a View event report link.

The report itself is the full record of who you interviewed, with filters for the rating your team gave each person. It has its own article, listed under What to do next.

Active events and live events

The same event row looks different once the event has started. Knowing which state you are looking at tells you what you can still change.

An active event is one you have registered for that has not started yet. This is the setup window: you can add and edit jobs, adjust capacity, invite interviewers, and share the listing. The interview location and slot length are the exceptions: you choose both before you publish your jobs, and neither can be changed after. The row shows the green Setup complete marker once nothing is outstanding.

A live event is one that is happening right now. The platform locks the setup so it cannot change underneath candidates who are already booked, and shifts you towards running the day from the lobby.

Plan the edits before the day

Because editing closes when an event starts, make your last changes to jobs and slot capacity while the event is still active. The interview location and slot length are fixed earlier, when you publish your jobs. Rescheduling and cancelling individual interviews is not affected: those stay available during a live event, from Interviews and from the lobby.

The left navigation

Five destinations run down the dark rail on the left, in the same order on every screen. The Collapse control at the top shrinks the rail to icons when you want more width for a table.

  • EventsYour upcoming and past events. The home base, covered above.
  • InterviewsRequests to answer, interviews booked, and interviews already held.
  • MessagesYour conversations with candidates.
  • AutomationsMessages that send on their own at set moments.
  • AnalyticsHow your interviews and events are performing.

Interviews

Everything scheduled or waiting on you, across every event. Three tabs carry counts: Upcoming for confirmed interviews, Pending for requests waiting on your reply, and Past for ones already held, where you record the Feedback rating and notes. An All hiring events dropdown narrows it to one event.

Messages

A two-pane inbox. Conversations are listed on the left under Inbox and Archived, filtered by event and job; selecting one opens the thread on the right with a message box at the bottom. You can also reach it from the Message link under any candidate's name.

Automations

Messages that send on their own as candidates move through your process. My automations lists what you have switched on, and five Automation presets underneath cover the common moments: a new interview request, an interview scheduled, a request declined, a missed interview, and a completed one.

Analytics

How your interviews and events are performing. A Date range control scopes the whole page, four summary cards sit under it, and Interviews over time plots any two metrics against each other. An Events table at the bottom breaks the same period down event by event.
