Before you buy

The three questions that come up most.

Are the candidates local?

Candidates are verified to be within approximately 20 miles of the event city — which is what makes an in-person interview location practical, not just a video call with a longer address field.

When does matching start after I register?

Candidate matching activates immediately and you will start receiving interview requests within a few hours.

How many interviews will I actually get?

It's tied to the package: Starter around 20, Growth around 60, Pro 100 or more — scheduled, not just matched.
