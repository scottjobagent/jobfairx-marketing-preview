03

Columns in the “December events” tab

  1. country: United States or Canada.
  2. event_id: our reference for each row, for example JFX26-1208-NY-Albany-VETE. The site can keep its own IDs.
  3. date and day_of_week: the local date, for example 2026-12-08 and Tuesday.
  4. market, city and state_or_province: the city as the site shows it, for example “Albany, NY”.
  5. event_type: Veterans, Entry-Level, Healthcare, Technology or Diversity, spelled like the site’s labels.
  6. event_title: for example “Albany Veterans Hiring Event”.
  7. start_time_local and end_time_local: 11:00 and 15:00.
  8. time_zone: the time zone label the site already shows for that city, for example EST, or PT for British Columbia.
  9. nothing_else_this_type_before_january: “yes” means that city has no other event of that type before its January event. It is for reference only. Please add all 810.
